## Prefilter policies

### Objectives:

At the end of this episode, I will be able to:

1. Explain the functions of the prefilter policy on the Cisco FTD
2. Perform the configuration of the prefilter policy


>Additional resources used during the episode can be obtained using the download link on the overview episode.

-----------------------------------------------------------
Remember the following points:
* The prefilter policy is run first before all other policies and rules on the FTD
* The prefilter policy can Fastpath traffic through the FTD, or it can drop traffic
* The prefilter policy can improve the performance of the FTD dramatically as it can ensure the device does not bog down performing security checks on traffic that should not even be permitted through the device
