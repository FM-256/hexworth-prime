## Policies and Rules of the FTD

### Objectives:

At the end of this episode, I will be able to:

1. Describe the various policies and rules that dictate the behavior of the FTD with data traffic
2. Describe the order of processing when it comes to these rules and policies  


>Additional resources used during the episode can be obtained using the download link on the overview episode.

-----------------------------------------------------------
Remember the following points:
* There is not just one simple policy that dictates the behavior of the FTD. It is many policies and rules working together.
* Here is a recap of the order:
 - Prefilter policy
 - Security Intelligence (IP)
 - Access Control (L3-L4)
 - Security Intelligence (DNS, URL)
 - Access Control (L7)
 - File Policy
 - Snort
