## Identity Policies

### Objectives:

At the end of this episode, I will be able to:

1. Describe the use of identity policies in Cisco firepower
2. Describe the need for identity policies
3. Explain the process of configuring identity policies in the Firepower


>Additional resources used during the episode can be obtained using the download link on the overview episode.

-----------------------------------------------------------
Remember the following points:
* You begin this configuration by defining your realm
* You can easily incorporate LDAP and AD directory stores
* You can configure no authentication whatsoever, passive authentication, or active authentication
