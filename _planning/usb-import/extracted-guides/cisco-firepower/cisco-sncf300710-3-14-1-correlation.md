## Correlation

### Objectives:

At the end of this episode, I will be able to:

1. Describe the two main jobs of a correlation policy
2. Describe how the configuration is performed  


>Additional resources used during the episode can be obtained using the download link on the overview episode.

-----------------------------------------------------------
Remember the following points:
* There are two main features of correlation policies that you can take advantage of - compliance white lists and custom correlation rules.
* Compliance white lists allow you to permit or deny flows based on a wide variety of criteria including operating system version.
* Correlation rules permit the creation of flexible conditions for a match (such as user identity). The responses can then vary thanks to the extensible nature of the FTD. You can install new module files with ease as we demonstrate in the episode.
