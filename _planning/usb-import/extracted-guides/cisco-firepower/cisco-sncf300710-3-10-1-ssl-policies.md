## SSL Policies

### Objectives:

At the end of this episode, I will be able to:

1. Describe the SSL policy approach in Cisco Firepower
2. Provide examples of using an SSL policy
3. Describe the configuration approach to SSL policies in the Cisco Firepower system


>Additional resources used during the episode can be obtained using the download link on the overview episode.

-----------------------------------------------------------
Remember the following points:
* The SSL policy dictates how encrypted traffic is handled by your FTD
* Remember, you can define actions based on the following 1) if the traffic is encrypted or not 2) whether your FTD can decrypt the traffic or not
* Once again, this is a policy that you assign by linking it to your access control policy
