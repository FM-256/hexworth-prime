## Intrusion Policies

### Objectives:

At the end of this episode, I will be able to:

1. Describe the intrusion policies possible in Cisco Firepower
2. Describe the configuration approach to intrusion policies in the Cisco Firepower


>Additional resources used during the episode can be obtained using the download link on the overview episode.

-----------------------------------------------------------
Remember the following points:
* Intrusion policies, like so many other policies, are tied in to the access control policy
* These policies are considered the "last line" of defense before a packet makes it to its destination
* If you have an inline configuration with your FTD, you can block traffic based on intrusion policy violations
