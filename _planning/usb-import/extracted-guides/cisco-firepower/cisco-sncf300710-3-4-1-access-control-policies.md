## Access Control Policies

### Objectives:

At the end of this episode, I will be able to:

1. Explain the functions and the importance of the Access Control Policy on the Cisco FTD
2. Configure an Access Control Policy on a Cisco FTD system


>Additional resources used during the episode can be obtained using the download link on the overview episode.

-----------------------------------------------------------
Remember the following points:
* The Access Control Policy is considered by most to be the "heart" of the FTD.
* Remember, you often will want to block some forms of traffic before the traffic even reaches this policy. You can do this with both the IP address Security Intelligence and the Prefilter policy.
* You must have one Access Control Policy per device. You cannot assign more than one ACP to a device, but you can take one policy and assign it to many devices.
* The Access Control Policy allows you to trigger so many other policy decisions.
