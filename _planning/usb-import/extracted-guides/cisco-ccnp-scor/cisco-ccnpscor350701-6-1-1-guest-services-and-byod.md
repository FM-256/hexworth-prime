## Guest Services and BYOD

### Objectives:

At the end of this episode, I will be able to:

1. Explain the importance of guest access and BYOD
2. Explain the main features of Cisco ISE


>Additional resources used during the episode can be obtained using the download link on the overview episode.

-----------------------------------------------------------
Remember the main topics of discussion in this episode:
* Cisco ISE supports wired, wireless, and VPN
* ISE supports auditing and posture
* ISE handles profiling, guest access, guest portals
* ISE does BYOD; MDM integration; certificate management; CTS
* ISE uses pxGrid; TACACS+; RADIUS; LDAP; AD
* ISE can be physical or virtual
