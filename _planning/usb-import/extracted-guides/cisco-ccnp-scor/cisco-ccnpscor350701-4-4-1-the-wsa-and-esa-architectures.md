Remember the main topics of discussion in this episode:
Here are the steps of the WSA process:
Step 1 – user initiates Web request
Step 2 – firewall (DG) redirects to the WSA
Step 3 – WSA checks request
Step 4 – WSA initiates web request
Step 5 – Web server replies to WSA
Step 6 – WSA sends reply content to client

Here are the steps for the ESA with inbound email:
Inbound Email
Step 1 – sender email to ronnie@acme123.com
Step 2 – DNS resolves to ESA IP address as mail server
Step 3 – email sent to ESA
Step 4 – after inspection, email sent to email server
Step 5 – email server sends to client

Here are the steps for the ESA for outbound mail:
Outbound Email
Step 1 – employee sends email
Step 2 – email server sends to ESA
Step 3 – email inspected by ESA
Step 4 – ESA performs DNS lookup
Step 5 – ESA sends to recipient
