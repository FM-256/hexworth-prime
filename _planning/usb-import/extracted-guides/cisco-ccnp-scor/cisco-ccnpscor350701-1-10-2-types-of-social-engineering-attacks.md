Remember that Phishing attacks have become so common, we need to identify the many variations of these attacks:
Phishing
* Deceptive
* Spear Phishing
* Whaling
* Vishing
* Smishing
* Pharming

Here are other social engineering attack types to be aware of:
* Tailgating
* Baiting
* Scareware
* Dumpster diving
* Watering hole
