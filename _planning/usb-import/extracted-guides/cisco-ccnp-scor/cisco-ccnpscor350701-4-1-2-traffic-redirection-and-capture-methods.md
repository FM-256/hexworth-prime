## Traffic Redirection and Capture Methods

### Objectives:

At the end of this episode, I will be able to:

1. Describe the two major options for traffic redirection in use today
2. Describe the overall configuration approach for WCCP


>Additional resources used during the episode can be obtained using the download link on the overview episode.

-----------------------------------------------------------
Remember the main topics of discussion in this episode:
* When you configure traffic redirection, you can operate in a stealth mode. Your clients will not see a proxy configuration in their browser.
* There are two main methods for this today. There is Policy Based Routing (PBR) and the Web Cache Communication Protocol (WCCP). WCCP is very simple to configure and is often used.
