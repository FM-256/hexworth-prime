## IKEv1 vs IKEv2

### Objectives:

At the end of this episode, I will be able to:

1. Explain the major differences between IKEv1 and IKEv2
2. Provide an example of usage for network telemetry


>Additional resources used during the episode can be obtained using the download link on the overview episode.

-----------------------------------------------------------
Remember the operational characteristics of IKEv1:
Phase 1 – main mode (6 messages) or aggressive mode (3 messages); used for the IKE SA

Phase 2 – quick mode (3 messages); IPsec SA

Here are the major enhancements to IKEv2:
* DoS protection
* Fewer round trips (2 messages)
* DPD
* Unidirectional authentication
