## Northbound and Southbound APIs of SDN

### Objectives:

At the end of this episode, I will be able to:

1. Describe SDN
2. Discuss the use of Northbound and Southbound APIs
3. Discuss the use of DNAC APIs


>Additional resources used during the episode can be obtained using the download link on the overview episode.

-----------------------------------------------------------
Remember the following points:
* The north and south references are in relation to the SDN controller of the network
* The APIs used today for the northbound type are often RESFUL APIs; and keep in mind the southbound APIs can also be RESTFUL
* You can practice and explore DNAC APIs with the DNAC always-on sandbox at Cisco DevNet
