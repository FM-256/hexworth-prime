## Configure and Verify the ESA
### Objectives:

At the end of this episode, I will be able to:

1. Describe the components of a typical email system  
2. Understand the components of the Cisco ESA
3. Describe the features supported


>Additional resources used during the episode can be obtained using the download link on the overview episode.

-----------------------------------------------------------
Remember the main topics of discussion in this episode:
* There are many potential components of an email system - MTA, MDA, MUA, MSA, IMAP, POP, DNS MX Record
* The most important for us are the MTA, IMAP, POP, DNS MX Record
* We configure ESA Listeners on the ESA in order to receiver mail
* There is a SenderBase for reputation scoring
* There is McAfee and Sophos antivirus support
* There are also outbreak filters, RATs (Recipient Address Tables), and Data Loss Prevention supported
