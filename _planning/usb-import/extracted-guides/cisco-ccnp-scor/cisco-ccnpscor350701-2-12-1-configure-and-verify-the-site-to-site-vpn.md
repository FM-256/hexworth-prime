## Configure and Verify the Site-to-Site VPN

### Objectives:

At the end of this episode, I will be able to:

1. Understand the concepts of the site to site VPN
2. Understand a configuration approach to a typical site to site VPN configuration


>Additional resources used during the episode can be obtained using the download link on the overview episode.

-----------------------------------------------------------
Remember the main topics of discussion in this episode:
* The DMVPN is just one of the many site to site VPN possibilities with Cisco Systems
* This DMVPN is ideal for hub and spoke deployments
* The DMVPN combines the tech of mGRE, NHRP, and IPsec
