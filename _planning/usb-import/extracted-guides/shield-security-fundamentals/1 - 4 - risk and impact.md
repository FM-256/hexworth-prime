## Risk and Impact  

At the end of this episode, I will be able to:    

1. Describe concepts related to risk and impact.    

Learner Objective: *Describe concepts related to risk and impact.*    

Description: In this episode, the learner will be concepts surrounding risk management and assessment as a part of organizational security.  

--------   

* Risk - the probability or likelihood of loss, exposure or damage to a company's information system or data.  
	+ Greater the risk = greater potential for loss or damage. 
	+ These risks can be deliberate and unauthorized breaches of security  
	+ These risks can be unintentional or accidental breaches of security  
* Breach - any incident that results in unauthorized access to computer data, applications, networks or devices  
* Impact - the extent to which a risk event could potentially affect an organization.    
* Risk management - identification, evaluation, prioritization and controlling of risks to an organization. 
* Risk assessment - a process to identify potential hazards, likelihood and consequences of these events. 
	+ Risk matrix - a tool used during a risk assessment to define the level of severity a risk event would have to an organization during planning.
	+ Impact analysis - a detailed study that seeks to predict the consequence that a risk event would have on an organization should it be realized.
* Risk management process \(according to the National Institute of Standards and Technology\)  
	+ Prepare - preparation of essential activities to manage security and privacy risks.   
	+ Categorize - categorize the system and information processed, stored and transmitted based on and impact analysis \(see Impact below\).   
	+ Select - select the set of controls to protect the system based on risk assessments.   
	+ Implement - implementation of the controls and documentation of how the controls are deployed.   
	+ Assess - determining if the controls are in place, operating as intended and producing the desired results.    
	+ Authorize - senior official\(s\) makes the risk-based decision to authorize the system \(to operate\).   
	+ Monitor - continuously monitoring control implementations and risks to the system.
