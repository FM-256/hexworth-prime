## Social Engineering    
At the end of this episode, I will be able to:    

1. Compare and contrast social engineering techniques.    

Learner Objective: *Compare and contrast social engineering techniques.*      

Description: In this episode, the learner will explore various methods used in social engineering.   

--------

* Social Engineering - a set of techniques using deception to manipulate an authorized user to disclose confidential information, commonly account information, passwords and banking information.
	+ Examples:  
		- Phishing - fradulent emails that are distributed to the general public in order to entice or convince a user to reveal personal information such as bank information, password, intellectual property.  
		- Spear phishing - this is a targeted phishing attack against a specific organization.  
		- Whaling - a phishing attack that targets high-profile employees of an organization such as senior management or C-level employees.
		- Baiting - the use of false promise to play on a user's greed or curiosity to steal personal information. 
		- Pretexting - a made-up scenario that is developed by a threat actor to steal a victim personal data.  
		- Tailgating - a attack in which a threat actor bypasses or seeks to bypass an access control system to a restricted area.
