##CRISCa- Governance and Risk Management Concepts

###Objectives:
At the end of this episode, I will be able to understand:

1. It all starts with reviewing existing practices and procedures
2. IT Risk management should be:
  * Comprehensive
  * Complete
  * Auditable
  * Justifiable
  * Compliant
  * Monitored
3. Risk Management is the coordinated activities to direct and control an enterprise in regards to risk
4. Risk is a challenge to achieving objectives
5. Risk Management lowers the chances of occurring risks and their impact
