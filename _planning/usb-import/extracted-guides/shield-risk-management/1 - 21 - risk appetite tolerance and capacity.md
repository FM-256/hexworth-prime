##CRISCa- Governance and Risk Management Concepts

###Objectives:
At the end of this episode, I will be able to understand:

1. Risk Appetite:
  * The amount of risk that an entity is willing to accept
  * The Owners or Board of Directors set the risk appetite for the company
  * This can also be delegated to senior management
  * Translates into standards and policies to maintain risk at the level of the risk appetite
2. Risk Capacity:
  * Risk capacity is the amount of loss an enterprise can take without being in danger of closing
  * Helps to set the risk appetite
3. Risk Tolerance:
  * The acceptable level of variation that management is willing to allow for any particular risk
  * This allows for occasional (and approved) deviations from the risk appetite
  * The risk tolerance level is still under the risk capacity level
  * Defined at the enterprise level and reflected in policies
4. Benefits of defining Risk Appetite and Capacity:
  * Supports risk-based decision making
  * Supports the understanding of how everything contributes to the overall risk profile
  * Supports the prioritization and approval process of risk response actions
  * Helps identify specific areas where a risk response should happen
5. Best case of communicating Risk appetite:
  * Consistent implementation
  * Effective monitoring and communication
  * Consistent understanding of risk appetite and related tolerance
