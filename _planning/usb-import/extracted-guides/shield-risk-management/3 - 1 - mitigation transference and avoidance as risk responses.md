##CRISCc - Risk Response and Reporting Effectively

###Objectives:
At the end of this episode, I will be able to understand:

1. The goal of Risk Response is to bring risk into alignment with the business risk appetite and tolerance
2. Risk Assessment report and Risk Register document the risk identified and analyzed/assessed
3. The recommendations made are guidelines
4. There are four responses: Acceptance, Mitigation, Transfer, Avoid
5. Mitigation of risk:
  * Actions taken to reduce the impact of a risk
  * Typically accomplished by installing controls
  * The goal is to reduce risk to an acceptable level, not to eliminate it
  * Generally less expensive than avoidance and transference
6. Transference of Risk:
  * Reduces loss by having another organization handle the impact
  * Commonly done through third-party insurance
  * Doesn’t mean the organization won’t feel the impact
  * Stakeholders rarely feel that the organization is not to blame
7. Avoidance of Risk:
  * Exiting the activities or conditions that give rise to risk
  * Current exposure is deemed unacceptable by senior management
  * Chosen when it is impossible to mitigate or transfer
  * Impact here is typically loss of opportunity
