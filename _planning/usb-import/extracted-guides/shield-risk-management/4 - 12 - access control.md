##CRISCd - Information Technology and Security Principles

###Objectives:
At the end of this episode, I will be able to understand:

1. Managing access to systems and data is one of the most difficult risk areas for a practitioner
2. In order to help with this, there is the concept of IAAA.
  * Identification
  * Authentication
  * Authorization
  * Accountability (Accounting)
3. Authentication is The process of validating an identity
  * Something you know
  * Something you have
  * Something you are
4. Changing passwords on a regular basis is a risk response for this part of IAAA
5. with Authorization, Authentication must occur first
6. Systems then provide appropriate authorized access
7. Proper use of the “least privilege” principle is vital here
8. Isolation is important. Authorization is typically granted for a limited period and only from certain devices or locations
9. Accountability is completed through auditing
10. Needs excellent systems logging or recording activity
11. Audit logs should NOT be changeable by operations staff (even admins)
