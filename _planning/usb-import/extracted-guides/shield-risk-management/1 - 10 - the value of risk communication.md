##CRISCa- Governance and Risk Management Concepts

###Objectives:
At the end of this episode, I will be able to understand:

1. Communication is key to successful Risk Management
2. Not all incidents need to be reported. However, lack of communication leads to unhealthy organizations
3. Benefits of good communication include:
  *More informed risk decisions
  *Greater awareness within the organization of risk
  *Transparency to customers and vendors
  *Consequences of poor risk communication:
  *Overconfidence in organizations abilities to manage risk
  *False sense of security
  *Decrease in value to stakeholders
  *Incorrect categorization of risk
4. What should be communicated in regards to risk:
  *Expectations from senior management
  *Current risk management capability
  *Status of all departments
