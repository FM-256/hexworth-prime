##CRISCa- Governance and Risk Management Concepts

###Objectives:
At the end of this episode, I will be able to understand:

1. Risk Management process, controls and principles
2. Risk management should not unduly affect business ops
3. Risk practitioners give information to the Risk owner to make wise decisions
4. Processes and controls can cause businesses to become slow and ineffective
5. Principles should consider:
  * Risk associated with process failures, economic trends, or acts of nature need to be considered
  * Any threat that prevents the enterprise from meeting its goals should be managed
  * Thus the need for guiding principles to help manage risk
  * Connect management of IT risk to business objectives
  * Align management of risk with ERM
  * Balance the costs and benefits of managing IT risk
  * Promote ethical and open communication of all IT risk
6. Controls and processes should consider:
  * Care and consideration must be taken when introducing processes and controls
  * Benefits and values must be properly communicated
  * Being transparent with controls and the need for them will limit staff “workarounds”
  * Try not to hamper productivity if possible
