##CRISCb- IT Risk Assessment Practices

###Objectives:
At the end of this episode, I will be able to understand:

1. When it comes to Threat modeling, Threats depend a lot on the mindset and approach of an attacker (Threat Actor)
2. Threat modeling is all about mapping methods, approaches, steps and techniques
3. Threat actors use different tools, different techniques
4. Threat Modeling, Abuse-case modeling is different than standard use-case modeling
5. Threat modeling is important as part of the risk analysis process as well
6. It’s important to understand which controls successfully defend against attacks. We put these answers in a Threat Profile
7. Threat Modeling methods:
  * STRIDE
  * PASTA
  * LINDDUN
  * Attack Trees
  * PnG
  * Trike
  * VAST
