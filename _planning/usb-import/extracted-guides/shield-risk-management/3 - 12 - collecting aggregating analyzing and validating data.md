##CRISCc - Risk Response and Reporting Effectively

###Objectives:
At the end of this episode, I will be able to understand:

1. For collecting and validating data, There are many sources of data available to a risk practitioner
2. Having data is valuable, but sifting through the large amounts can be difficult
3. Incorrect analysis can lead to a bad decision or suggestion
4. There are several methods to find hidden risk factors:
  * CAUSE AND EFFECT – Predictive or diagnostic analytical tool used to find root causes and identify potential risk.  Uses Fishbone Diagram

  * FAULT TREE ANALYSIS – Technique that provides a systematic description of the combination of possible occurrences in a system. IMPORTANT – Combines hardware failures and human failures.  Constructed by relating the sequences of events that, individually or in a combination , could lead to the top level event (Most serious and undesirable outcome).

  * SENSITIVITY ANALYSIS – Think single factor sensitivity.  Quantitative analysis technique helps identify the single most impactful risk factor.  Examines the extent to which the uncertainty of each element affects the target object when everything else is held at baselines.  Creates a Tornado Diagram.
5. Risk Data Collection And Extraction Tools And Techniques:
  * Prior Risk assessments
  * Audit and incident reports
  * Logs
  * Security Information and Event Management
  * Integrated test facilities
  * External Sources
