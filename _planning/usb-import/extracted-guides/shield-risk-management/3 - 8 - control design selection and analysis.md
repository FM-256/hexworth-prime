##CRISCc - Risk Response and Reporting Effectively

###Objectives:
At the end of this episode, I will be able to understand:

1. When it comes to design, selection and analysis of controls, first step in designing controls is to be aware of the current state of control environment
2. Current risk levels are essential to provide the reference point for gap analysis
3. Audit reports are too much. Practitioners should be careful in requesting audits. Look at any already completed audit reports
4. Once shortcomings are identified, next step is to design or select controls to close the gap
5. Decide between proactive controls (safeguards) and reactive controls (countermeasures)
6. Effective controls prevent, detect or contain an incident
7. Sometimes you will have to use compensating controls
8. Compensating controls must be effective and protect the identified assets or processes.  These must not be duplicated, redundant or increase the existing risk surface.  These controls will combine with existing controls to offset the risk that couldn’t be addressed directly
