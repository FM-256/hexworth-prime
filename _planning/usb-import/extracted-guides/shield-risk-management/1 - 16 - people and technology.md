##CRISCa- Governance and Risk Management Concepts

###Objectives:
At the end of this episode, I will be able to understand:

1. One of the biggest assets of an enterprise is the people
2. Enterprises are vulnerable to the loss of a key employee
3. Cross-training, proper communication, financial incentives can be vital to retaining talent
4. Technology changes all the time
5. Equipment needs updating or replacing constantly
6. Systems that are scheduled for disposal might contain sensitive data
7. Data retention is important for regulations and legal reasons
