##CRISCb- IT Risk Assessment Practices

###Objectives:
At the end of this episode, I will be able to understand:

1. When performing Risk Identification, seek to identify loss-event scenarios affecting your business goals and objectives
2. Risk practitioners should work closely with business process owners
3. Risk practitioners should consult with IT and cybersecurity experts to understand technological risk
4. Risk events are specific occurrences that create an impact in a business. These are not threats
5. Risk events = Threats with attached probability and impact on a company’s asset(s)
6. Risk practitioners should first understand their current and future environment
7. Risk Identification Terms:
  * Asset
  * Impact
  * Likelihood
  * Threat
  * Threat actor
  * Threat vector
  * Vulnerability
