##CRISCb- IT Risk Assessment Practices

###Objectives:
At the end of this episode, I will be able to understand:

1. Risk Scenarios are descriptions of possible threat events that have an uncertain impact
2. Used to conceptualize risk
3. Each scenario should be related to a business objective
4. Can be based on past events, but should also look to future events
5. Approaches to Risk Scenario Development:
  * Top-Down
  * Bottom-Up
6. Components used in Risk Scenario Development:
  * Actors
  * Threat Types/Events
  * Asset/Resource
  * Estimated Frequency/Impact
7. Risk Scenarios are useful to communicate the potential probability and impact
8. These things are difficult to predict with great precision
9. Using F.A.I.R. or H.A.R.M. can help with creating multiple simulations and map qualitative statements to quantitative values
