##CRISCa- Governance and Risk Management Concepts

###Objectives:
At the end of this episode, I will be able to understand:

1. There are Roles in Risk Management
2. These Roles (along with their RACI attribute) are:
  Risk Manager – Responsible for ensuring that the risk management functions are carried out to support the enterprise goals and objectives
  Risk analyst – Responsible for the analysis, evaluation and assessment of identified threats faced by the enterprise
  Risk owner – The individual in whom the enterprise has invested the authority and ACCOUNTABILITY for making risk-based decisions and who owns the loss associated with a realized risk scenario
  Control Owner – The individual accountable for ensuring controls are designed, implemented and operating as planned to keep risk at an acceptable level. This may also be the risk owner. Includes budgeting, staffing, design implementation and monitoring of controls.
  Control Stewards – The people who are responsible for the routine management and maintenance of controls on the behalf of the control owner.
  SMEs – These are the experts who have intimate knowledge and expertise in particular areas. Good for consulting to gain better and more accurate estimates of risk.
