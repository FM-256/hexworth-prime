##CRISCa- Governance and Risk Management Concepts

###Objectives:
At the end of this episode, I will be able to understand:

1. Companies must comply with laws and regulations in their jurisdictions
2. Some laws and regulations can be challenging to a business
3. Some laws and regulations contradict each other
4. If an Enterprise is global, set up global polices and then have regional or country-based exceptions as necessary
5. Types of Regulations include:
  * GDPR
  * PCI-DSS
  * HIPAA
  * SOX
6. Risk practitioners should work with legal counsel when building reporting
7. Must have appropriate controls in place
8. Compliance is a risk decision
9. Risk is often impacted by ethics
10. Ethics apply to how people believe that they have been treated
