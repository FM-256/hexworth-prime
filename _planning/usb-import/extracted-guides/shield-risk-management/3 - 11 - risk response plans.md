##CRISCc - Risk Response and Reporting Effectively

###Objectives:
At the end of this episode, I will be able to understand:

1. Risk Response plans are quite large
2. There are several responses to risk that can be utilized
3. Practitioners are consultants that assist the risk owners in deciding the response based on many different factors
4. Risk treatment planning should be run as a project
5. End date of the project determines the critical path of the project
6. Risk Practitioners should present the final deliverable to risk owners
