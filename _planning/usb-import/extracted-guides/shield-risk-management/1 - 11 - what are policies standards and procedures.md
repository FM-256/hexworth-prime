##CRISCa- Governance and Risk Management Concepts

###Objectives:
At the end of this episode, I will be able to understand:

1. Policies provide direction on acceptable behaviors and actions
2. Standards and procedures support the requirements of policies
3. All three are needed for risk management
4. Standards are:
  *Mandatory requirements
  *Code of Practice
  *Might be based on external standards (ISO, etc)
  *Might be based internally
5. Procedures are:
  *More granular than standards
  *Support implementation of standards
  *The “How to” for standards
6. Exceptions can occur and must be dealt with
7. Exceptions MUST be documented
8. Exceptions are only allowed through a formal process
