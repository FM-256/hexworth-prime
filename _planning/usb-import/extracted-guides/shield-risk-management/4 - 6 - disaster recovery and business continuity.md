##CRISCd - Information Technology and Security Principles

###Objectives:
At the end of this episode, I will be able to understand:

1. Risk management considers the full spectrum of risk from minor to catastrophic impacts
2. The business continuity process creates a Business Continuity Plan (BCP) to help with mitigating the risk
3. BCPs provide sufficient levels of functionality in operations when disaster strikes
4. BCPs come out of the Business Impact Analysis
5. The BIA establishes the recovery point objectives (RPOs) and recovery time objectives (RTOs)
6. Environments with low RTOs have high availability systems
7. Environments with low RPOs have high availability data architectures
8. Disaster recovery refers to the recovery of business and IT services following a disaster
9. Completed within a defined schedule and budget
10. Flows from RTOs established by BIA and built into BCP
11. There should be a primary response and alternate for every activity
