##CRISCd - Information Technology and Security Principles

###Objectives:
At the end of this episode, I will be able to understand:

1. Technology is constantly changing and growing everywhere
2. Pressure to implement new technologies to “keep up” with the competition is huge
3. This can cause an organization to lose track of its primary goals and objectives
4. Some Emerging Technologies to be aware of from a risk perspective:
  * Omnipresent connectivity
  * Bring Your Own Device (BYOD)
  * Internet of Things (IoT)
  * Deepfakes and AI
  * Blockchain
