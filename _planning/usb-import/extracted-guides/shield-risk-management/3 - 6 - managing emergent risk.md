##CRISCc - Risk Response and Reporting Effectively

###Objectives:
At the end of this episode, I will be able to understand:

1. When it comes to emerging risk, remember that all risk is fundamentally dynamic.Thus, risk management must be continuous
2. Technology, configurations, and personnel are constantly changing
3. This means that controls are constantly being modified
4. When it comes to vulnerabilities and controls, any changes with controls need to be carefully assessed
5. Deploying new controls or modifying existing controls is risky
6. Sometimes the “change” is worse than the consequence
7. Adoption of new technology must be a formal process to help with risk management
8. Informal adoption is called “Shadow IT”
9. Risk practitioners should use automated scanners for systems and networks
10. New technologies can be helpful or detrimental to existing controls
