##CRISCa- Governance and Risk Management Concepts

###Objectives:
At the end of this episode, I will be able to understand:

1. Reviewing the business process:
  * Examines the effectiveness of the business in meeting its goals
  * Requires input from knowledgeable people from all departments
  * You may also bring in external experts (consultants)
2. The steps to review a business process are:
  * Document and evaluate the current processes
  * Identify any potential changes
  * Schedule and implement the changes
  * Gain feedback and evaluate
3. Remember that with Business Process Reviews:
  * Outdated business processes introduce risk
  * Periodic review of critical processes should be mandatory
  * Updates should be performed as appropriate
