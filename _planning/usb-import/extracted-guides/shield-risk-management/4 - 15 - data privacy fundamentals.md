##CRISCd - Information Technology and Security Principles

###Objectives:
At the end of this episode, I will be able to understand:

1. Practitioners should understand that privacy is similar to confidentiality
2. Difference between privacy and confidentiality: Rights regarding the handling and retention of a subject’s PII (Personal Identifiable Information)
3. These rights are still valid even if the data is not in the subject’s control
4. Data privacy concepts to know for exam:
  * INFORMED CONSENT – Data subject to privacy regulations should be collected, used and retained with the informed consent.  A signed consent form is typically attached.

  * PIA – These identify and manage risk related to privacy whenever personal information is collected.  PIAs typically cover how information is used, shared and maintained.  Similar to a risk assessment.

  * MINIMIZATION – Risk practioners should understand that the MORE data that you keep around…the more of a risk surface area you have.

  * DESTRUCTION – Data should generally be destroyed once its reason to exist is over.  However, some data must be retained for a certain time due to legal and regulatory reasons.
