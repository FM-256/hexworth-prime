##CRISCc - Risk Response and Reporting Effectively

###Objectives:
At the end of this episode, I will be able to understand:

1. Any variations from the norm or desired outcome creates risk
2. Formal approaches to configuration, release, and change management helps
3. Controls can only do so much
4. Technology changes rapidly
5. Standardizing system configurations can reduce complexity
6. Risk practitioners should talk with configuration management
7. Anytime you release a new function or product you introduce risk
8. Understanding how internal development works is vital
9. Verification and protection prior to deployment must align to risk appetite
10. Policies and procedures must be in place for exceptions
11. Enterprises encounter circumstances where usual practices are unsuitable
12. All exceptions must be formally documented and approved by a senior manager
13. Change happens, which means risk
14. Needs effective change control to help manage risk
15. Control Advisory Boards (CABs) are helpful
16. Change needs to be a formal process to reduce risk
