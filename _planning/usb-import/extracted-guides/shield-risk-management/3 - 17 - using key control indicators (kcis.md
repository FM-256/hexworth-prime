##CRISCc - Risk Response and Reporting Effectively

###Objectives:
At the end of this episode, I will be able to understand:

1. Certain indicators show the control effectiveness to the team
2. The indicator should quantify how well a specific control is working
3. The goal of a KCI is to track performance of control actions relative to tolerances
4. Examples of KCIs can be:
  * Phishing emails blocked or not blocked
  * Admins with right to both test and production environments
  * Unsolicited prompts for second-factor authentication
5. Each KCI has its own tolerances based on the asset is protects
6. KCIs are correlated with the risk they are meant to mitigate
7. This correlation also means that all KCIs are themselves risk indicators
