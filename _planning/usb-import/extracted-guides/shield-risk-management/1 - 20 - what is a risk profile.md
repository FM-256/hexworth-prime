##CRISCa- Governance and Risk Management Concepts

###Objectives:
At the end of this episode, I will be able to understand:

1. Risk Profiles are Based on the overall risk posture of the enterprise
2. Reflected in its attention to monitoring controls
3. Profiles Help the enterprise have proactive identification, analysis and response to risk
4. Key input to developing a risk culture
5. Changing the profile must be done carefully.
6. Risk at the top level of an organization is the result of many individual risks
7. Risk posture may be affected by cascading effects of minor risks
8. IT risk management objectives should be reviewed consistently
9. Risk is owned by management but risk practitioners work with them to make them aware of those risks
