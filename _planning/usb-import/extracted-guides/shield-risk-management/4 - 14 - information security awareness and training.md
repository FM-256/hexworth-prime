##CRISCd - Information Technology and Security Principles

###Objectives:
At the end of this episode, I will be able to understand:

1. Familiarity with technology can help reduce operation training costs but increase security risk
2. Threat actors know that human users are the weakest link
3. Social media has made people more willing to share information openly
4. Inadequate SoD and Least Privilege has also caused risk
5. Best practices focus on Practitioners needing to review the scope of information being trained
6. Topics to cover:
  * Social engineering
  * How to alert internal security
  * Policies and regulatory requirements specific to certain job roles
7. Resilient people help create resilient organizations
