##CRISCd - Information Technology and Security Principles

###Objectives:
At the end of this episode, I will be able to understand:

1. Systems exist in all organizations
2. There is a lifecycle associated with systems and their development called SDLC
3. SDLC can stand for Software Development Lifecycle as well
4. A SDLC is a methodology intended to support effective project management
5. Key security tasks performed as part of SDLCs:
  * Document the risk
  * Security categorization
  * What are the CIA requirements?
  * Privacy impact assessment
  * Vendor/Supplier risk assessment
  * Waterfall or Agile methodology
