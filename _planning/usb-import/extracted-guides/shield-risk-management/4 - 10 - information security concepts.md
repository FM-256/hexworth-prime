##CRISCd - Information Technology and Security Principles

###Objectives:
At the end of this episode, I will be able to understand:

1. The goal of risk management is to make sure technology used in organizations is:
  * Adequately protected
  * Reliable
  * Secure
2. Risk based concerns include:
  * Creating policies
  * System identification for backups
  * Assigning Risk ownership
  * Reviewing regulatory needs
  * Training your staff and admins
3. Practitioners should understand that every system needs a system owner
4. Most owners delegate down to operations
5. Transference of Responsibility DOES NOT mean transferring accountability
6. There can be different cultures within an organization due to size and complexity of groups/teams
