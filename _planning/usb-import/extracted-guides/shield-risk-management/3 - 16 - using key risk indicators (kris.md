##CRISCc - Risk Response and Reporting Effectively

###Objectives:
At the end of this episode, I will be able to understand:

1. Risk Indicators are used to measure risk levels in comparison to defined risk thresholds
2. KRIs are risk indicators that are highly relevant and possess a high probability of prediction accuracy
3. Examples of KRIs
  * Number of unauthorized equipment detected
  * Number of times SLAs exceeded the thresholds
  * Average time to deploy security patches
4. KRIs should be carefully and sparingly selected
5. Besides SMART metrics, KRIs should consider factors like: Balance and Root Cause
6. KRIs are more dynamic than KPIs
7. KRIs should be optimized to make sure that the correct data is being collected and reported on
