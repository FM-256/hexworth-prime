##CRISCa- Governance and Risk Management Concepts

###Objectives:
At the end of this episode, I will be able to understand:

1. There are three elements in Risk Culture:
  *Behavior toward taking risk – How much risk does the enterprise believe that it can absorb, and what specific risk is it willing to take?
  *Behavior toward policy compliance – To what extent do people within the enterprise embrace or comply with policy?
  *Behavior toward negative outcomes – How does the enterprise deal with losses, missed opportunities, and other negative outcomes? Do they learn from them and try to adjust or do they play the blame game without treating the root cause?
2. When Risk Management doesn't take culture into consideration you will encounter:
  *Misalignment between Risk Appetite and actual behavior
  *Existence of a Blame Culture
