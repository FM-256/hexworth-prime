##CRISCd - Information Technology and Security Principles

###Objectives:
At the end of this episode, I will be able to understand:

1. Project management is all about communication, managing stakeholders, and risk
2. Large amounts of risk are involved in scheduling, cost and quality
3. Many IT projects fail due to unclear or changing requirements, problems with technology
4. Project management methodologies from waterfall to Agile are well established
5. Common risk responses:
 * Change control boards
 * Project task prioritization
 * Risk management process
6. Large projects can be aggregated into Programs to help with resource management
