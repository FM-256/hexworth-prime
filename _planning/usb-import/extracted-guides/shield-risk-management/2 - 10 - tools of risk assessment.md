##CRISCb- IT Risk Assessment Practices

###Objectives:
At the end of this episode, I will be able to understand:

1. Risk practitioners should use tools wisely and choose appropriately
2. A consistent assessment technique should be used when the goal is to produce results compared over time
3. Practitioners should not use as many techniques as possible nor only one
4. The Risk Register Consolidates risk data into one place and enables tracking
  * Shows impact/probability
  * Should document the organization’s entire risk
  * Risk register drives the management of risk
5. With risk ranking, Results of risk assessment need to be prioritized
6. Weighted score derived from all the components of risk
7. Senior management should be involved in the definition of weights
8. Make sure this information is in the Risk Register
9. Risk maps are used after qualitative and quantitative analysis is complete
  * Scenarios are considered in the context of risk appetite and risk tolerance
  * Sometimes called a Probability and Impact Matrix
  * Risk maps help the practitioner assess what  the appropriate level of response should be
10. Risk Assessments consider Risk Ownership, Risk Documentation and Risk Exclusions
