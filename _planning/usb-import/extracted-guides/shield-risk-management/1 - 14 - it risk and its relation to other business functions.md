##CRISCa- Governance and Risk Management Concepts

###Objectives:
At the end of this episode, I will be able to understand:

1. Taking on too much risk or exceeding risk capacity can be harmful
2. Senior Management is RESPONSIBLE for establishing risk appetite and tolerances
3. Risk practitioners must understand that IT is connected to many other business functions
4. IT risk management is closely linked to business continuity
5. Risk management attempts to reduce risk to an acceptable level
6. As part of this, identifying potential disasters or major incidents is vital for the BCP
7. Risk is a part of the Audit process:
  * Audit is all about insuring compliance with policy and/or regulations
  * IS audits should be conducted by trained professionals
  * Risk practitioners should review the relationship between IS auditor and area being audited
8. Controls are created or chosen to mitigate a risk
9. Controls need to be reviewed continuously
10. Risk owners should be presented with some options to choose from
11. Controls should be judged by: Efficiency, Effectiveness, Exposure
