##CRISCa- Governance and Risk Management Concepts

###Objectives:
At the end of this episode, I will be able to understand:

1. When it comes to an organization's culture, it is impactful on IT Risk Management
2. Culture drives the behaviors of personnel
3. All employees should be aware of their risk culture
4. Subcultures can exist within an organization
5. Risk awareness is a powerful tool in creating the culture and forming ethics
6. When it comes to Risk Awareness:
  *Risk is well understood,
  *IT Risk issues are identifiable,
  *Enterprise understands what’s required to manage risk,
  *Awareness education and training are key,
  *Awareness of InfoSec policies, standards and procedures are necessary,
  *Different training for different levels (i.e. management vs. senior mgmt)
