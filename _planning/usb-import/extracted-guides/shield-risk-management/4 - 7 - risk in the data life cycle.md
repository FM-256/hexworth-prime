##CRISCd - Information Technology and Security Principles

###Objectives:
At the end of this episode, I will be able to understand:

1. Data typically follows a six-phase lifecycle
2. There are many caveats to the lifecycle
3. For example, creation can be original collection or synthesis from other data
4. Data is one of business’ most valuable assets
5. To protect data you must first identify it in terms of use and then classify it in terms of value
6. Data validation can be done with a whitelist or blacklist
7. Most important is control over the permissions and authorization levels
