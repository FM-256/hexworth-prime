##CRISCb- IT Risk Assessment Practices

###Objectives:
At the end of this episode, I will be able to understand:

1. Business Impact Analysis (BIA) is a process to help establish disaster recovery and continuity requirements
2. Comprehensive BIA works to establish the escalation of loss over time
3. BIA helps the risk practitioner in proposing appropriate risk responses
4. BIA helps senior management select appropriate recovery strategies
5. BIA concentrates on the activities and resources needed for the most critical services
6. Recovery Time Objectives (RTO) are established
7. Key processes and resources can be prioritized and put into the business continuity plan (BCP)
8. BIA and Risk management are typically performed in close coordination
9. There are distinctions between the two concepts:
  * BIA is about business continuity
  * Risk assessment is about understanding the threats facing the business
