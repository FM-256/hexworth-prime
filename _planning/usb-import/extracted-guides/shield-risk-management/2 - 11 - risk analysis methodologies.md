##CRISCb- IT Risk Assessment Practices

###Objectives:
At the end of this episode, I will be able to understand:

1. for quantitative risk analysis methodologies:
  * Uses mathematical models to simulate potential outcomes
  * Often referenced by financial or time values
  * Relies on quality data to make accurate predictions
  * Suitable for cost-benefit analysis
  * Uses EMV (Expected Monetary Value) and ALE (Annual Loss Expectancy)
2. for qualitative risk analysis methodologies:
  * Considers the “quality” of the risk.
  * Uses P/I matrix based on subjective H-M-L values on probability and impact
  * Relies heavily on experience of experts
  * Typically based on scenarios or descriptions of possible risk events
3. Causes of risk are not always obvious
  * Root Cause Analysis asks a series of questions to get to the root of the issue
  * Uses a “Fishbone”/Ishikawa diagram
  * Helps with risk assessment to understand the cause and effect
