##CRISCa- Governance and Risk Management Concepts

###Objectives:
At the end of this episode, I will be able to understand:

1. Key Risk Concepts include:
  All parties need to understand the taxonomy of risk Likelihood (Probability) and Consequences (Impact)
  Threats vs. Opportunities
  Vulnerabilities and Attacks
2. Using Risk Scenarios helps understand the impact to:
  Response costs Legal (fines, penalties, etc.)
  Damaged Reputation
  Health and Safety Productivity
3. Understanding Impact helps to make it:
  Easier for Managers to assign monetary value
  Easier for Managers to make risk response decisions
