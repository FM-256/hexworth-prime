##CRISCc - Risk Response and Reporting Effectively

###Objectives:
At the end of this episode, I will be able to understand:

1. Every risk needs an owner at a management level
2. Risk owners should be senior executive or manager of a business process/function
3. Risk practitioners communicate to risk owners the options for risk response for their decision
4. Ownership is all about accountability (RACI)
5. Risk owners must have authority, budget, and mandate to select risk responses
6. Every risk should have a SINGLE owner
7. Controls should be owned by the owner of the risk that it mitigates
8. Risk and control owners must be documented in the risk register
9. Risk owners need to drive the risk management activities
10. Desired outcomes must be communicated clearly to the risk practitioner
11. Aggregate risk is evaluated against the strategic risk appetite
