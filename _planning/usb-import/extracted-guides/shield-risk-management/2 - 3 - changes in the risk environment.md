##CRISCb- IT Risk Assessment Practices

###Objectives:
At the end of this episode, I will be able to understand:

1. Risk changes constantly
2. Organizations and Technologies change all the time
3. This changes the risk environment
4. Risk practitioners should constantly monitor the business and external reports (CERT)
5. Emerging threats should be identified, analyzed, evaluated, assessed and reported
6. Maintaining operational integrity Requires procedures such as incident management, change management, etc.
7. Consistent and sustainable operations LESSEN risk
8. Well defined procedures help businesses achieve their goals
9. IT departments need to be aware of changes in the industry
10. Risk practitioners should assess the maturity of the IT Department
11. Flexibility is key
12. Remember with Forecasting Risk:
  * Risks can occur as the result of preexisting conditions
  * Risk events can also cascade from one another
  * The greatest threat to an organization is failure to learn from past events and forecast accurately based on those events
