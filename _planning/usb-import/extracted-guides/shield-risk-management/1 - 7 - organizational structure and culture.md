##CRISCa- Governance and Risk Management Concepts

###Objectives:
At the end of this episode, I will be able to understand:

1. Structure and Culture of an Organization is critical to a risk management program
2. The Enterprise should have mandates on participation
3. Behavior and attitude towards risk is part of the culture
4. Collaboration and sharing of information is vital for success
5. Risk management is a core part of corporate governance
6. This requires understanding of the organization’s culture
7. There are many types of cultures:
  Vulnerable – Don’t care
  Reactive – Blame Culture
  Compliant – Compliance culture, Proactive – Ownership culture, Resilient – Way of Life culture
