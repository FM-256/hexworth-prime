##CRISCa- Governance and Risk Management Concepts

###Objectives:
At the end of this episode, I will be able to understand:

1. The Three Lines of Defense are implemented by many organizations
2. Identifies business functions between risk owner and risk practitioner – Specifies roles
  * First Line – Operational Management
  * Second Line – Risk and Compliance Functions
  * Third Line - Audit
3. First Line - Operational Management
  * Implemented by the business unit
  * Ensures the conductive control environment
  * Expected to be fully aware of risk factors
  * Able to execute effective internal control in their business units
  * First Line – Operational Managers are responsible for managing risk
4. Second Line - Risk and Compliance functions
  * Comprised of Risk Management and Compliance Functions
  * Focused to ensure that first-line defenses are properly designed
  * This is where enterprise buy-in occurs
5. Third Line - Audit
  * Gaining the appropriate level of assurances to senior management
  * Needs independent and objective reviews
  * Reviews and evaluates the design and implementation of risk management
6. Risk Practitioner works across the first two lines of defense within an organization
7. Auditors work in the third-line of defense. However, the auditor will give information TO the risk practitioner to help with delivering information to senior management and the board
