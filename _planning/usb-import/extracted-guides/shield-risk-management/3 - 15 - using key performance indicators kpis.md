##CRISCc - Risk Response and Reporting Effectively

###Objectives:
At the end of this episode, I will be able to understand:

1. Key Performance indicators measure how well a process is performing in terms of its stated goal
2. Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) measure activity goals
3. For example: a KPI may indicate that an error rate of 5% is acceptable
4. The risk attached would be if the KPI showed something over 5%
5. KPIs are based on SMART metrics
  * Specific
  * Measurable
  * Attainable
  * Relevant
  * Time bound
