##CRISCd - Information Technology and Security Principles

###Objectives:
At the end of this episode, I will be able to understand:

1. Virtualization is the ability to create operating systems out of files located on a physical computer
2. Due to the large computing power of a physical machine, you can produce multiple "virtual machines"
3. Virtualization is what enabled cloud technologies to be created.
4. Clouds come in Public, Private and Hybrid forms
5. There can be increased risk by using Cloud technologies due to the fact that your data might be on many different machines that are not under your direct control
6. Due consideration must be made when utilizing these technologies based on the risk appetite and tolerances of an organization
