##CRISCa- Governance and Risk Management Concepts

###Objectives:
At the end of this episode, I will be able to understand:

1. Projects often fail due to lack of proper risk management
2. Failure of an IT project presents risk to an enterprise
3. Proper identification of risk for a project will increase chances of success
4. Change risk understands that Risk is not static
5. Technology, regulations, processes, etc. change all the time
6. Risk practitioners must keep a continuous eye on risk
7. Change Advisory Boards (CABs) should communicate and identify risk
