##CRISCd - Information Technology and Security Principles

###Objectives:
At the end of this episode, I will be able to understand:

1. Computers abilities to communicate to one another is fundamental to Information Systems (IS)
2. Most modern networks are digital which are more resilient than analog
3. Networks are special in the world of IT risk management.
4. Because they move data back and forth, they are prime targets by threat actors
5. Networks use signals to transmit data in small pieces that are put back together at the receiving end
6. The most important protocol to understand is TCP/IP
7. TCP/IP predates the OSI model
8. The premise is still the same in that you move down the model to transmit and back up as you receive it
9. While wireless is hugely popular, physical cabling is still very useful
10. Software base networking terms:
  * Domain Name System (DNS)
  * Dynamic Host Configuration Protocol (DHCP)
  * Virtual Local Area Networks (VLANs)
  * Quality of Service (QoS)
