##CRISCa- Governance and Risk Management Concepts

###Objectives:
At the end of this episode, I will be able to understand:

1. IT Risk Management gives better oversight of assets by:
  Reduces or minimizes losses
  Identifies threats, vulnerabilities and consequences
  Prioritizes risk response efforts
  Increased probability of project success
  Improved decision making by senior management
2. IT Risk Management is part of overall business risk
3. IT Risk Management should be guided by an IT Risk strategy that aligns with business goals and objectives
4. IT Risk management needs formal definitions, documentation, monitoring
5. The types of IT Risk
6. The importance of Management support
7. IT Risk Management should align with business goals and objectives
8. The Risk Management Lifecycle is:
  IT Risk Identification
  IT Risk Assessment/Analysis
  IT Risk Response
  IT Risk Monitoring and Reporting
