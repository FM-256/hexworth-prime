##CRISCc - Risk Response and Reporting Effectively

###Objectives:
At the end of this episode, I will be able to understand:

1. When utilizing third-party risk management, Outsourcing to consultants/experts is a form of risk transfer
2. Must watch out for ownership of data issues
3. It requires excellent legal/contract management
4. Outsourcing does not offload the ownership or liability of a risk owner
5. Concerns to remember:
  * Hiring and training practices
  * Time to notify of incidents
  * Liability for non-compliance
  * Location of data
  * Handling of data after contract ends
6. Mitigation can occur by using SLAs
