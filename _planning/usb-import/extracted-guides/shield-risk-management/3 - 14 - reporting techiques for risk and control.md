##CRISCc - Risk Response and Reporting Effectively

###Objectives:
At the end of this episode, I will be able to understand:

1. With reporting techniques for controls, communication is vital
2. Practitioners are expected to give regular reports to management and the board of directors on the status of the risk plan
3. Effectiveness of control monitoring depends on:
  * Skill of the Data analyst
  * Quality of the data
  * Timeliness
4. Heat maps are graphical representation of risk distribution across a particular scope
5. Useful as a means of expressing the effectiveness of controls
6. Helps illustrate the underlying data in a visually attractive manner
7. Scorecards are basically a grading card for aggregating performance
8. Modern businesses use balanced scorecards to align the risk indicators alongside the 4 areas of the scorecard
9. Remember that scorecards are snapshots in time
10. Dashboards are data presented sequentially with distinct indicators
11. Typically shows data in real time, but not always
12. Best choice when presenting to senior management
