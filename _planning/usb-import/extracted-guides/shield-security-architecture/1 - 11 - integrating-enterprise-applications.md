### External Resources:

Integrating enterprise applications

  What are considerations when integrating enterprise applications? -

   • Customer Relationship Management (CRM) - provide basic customer purchasing
   metrics, sales projections, & communication services

   • Enterprise Resource Planning (ERP) - monitor the day-to-day operations of an
   enterprise & reports on the status of various resources & activities

   • Configuration Management Database (CMDB) - tracks & stores information
   pertaining to assets & components, such as software, hardware, policies, &
   personnel, as well as the relationships between these components

   • Content Management System (CMS) - create, manage, & modify content on a
   website


   What are Integration Services? -

   • Directory Services – authenticate access to all the resources available
   within a network

   • Domain Name System (DNS) – hierarchical naming system for entities connected
   to a network

   • Service-Oriented Architecture (SOA) – common method for integrating
   services, such as web-based applications, so that they can access data from
   a wide range of sources

   • Enterprise Service Bus (ESB) –  enables integration & communication between
   applications; used to redirect & convert messages from one format to another;
   contain endpoints, which are the logical addresses of connected services
