Security considerations - sectors

 What do you need to know about ICS/SCADA by Sector? -

 Energy - power generation & distribution as well as Oil & Gas, public utilities
 including water/sewage, gas, & public services such as transportation networks

 Industrial - mining & refining raw materials, including hazardous high heat &
 pressure furnaces, presses, centrifuges, & pumps

 Manufacturing - creation of components & assembling them into finished products
 & controlling automated production systems, such as forges, mills, & assembly
 lines

 Logistics - automated transport & lift systems plus sensors for component tracking

 Facilities - automated heating, ventilation, & air conditioning (HVAC), lighting
 & security systems

 Healthcare - keep track of patients, medical equipment, & supplies, as well as
 environmental controls
