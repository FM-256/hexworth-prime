Policies and security practices

 Risk Appetite vs. Risk Tolerance -

 Risk Appetite – How an organization will address identified risks in relation
 to business objectives.  Can be driven/proscribed by regulatory compliance.

 Risk Tolerance – The thresholds that separate different levels of risk, such
 as money, impact, scope, time, compliance, and/or privacy. The maximum level
 of risk acceptable in order to achieve a goal.

 Tradeoff analysis – How decisions are made by comparing potential benefits to
 potential risks, and determining a course of action based on adjusting factors
 that contribute to each area.

    • The Software Engineering Institute (SEI) at Carnegie Mellon University
    developed the Architecture Tradeoff Analysis Method (ATAM), which allows
    formal evaluation of architectures based upon the analysis of risks and
    desired outcomes.


 Additional Quantitative Measures -

  • Separation of duties – Checks & Balances

  • Job rotation – Visibility & Cross-training

  • Mandatory vacation – Visibility & Verification

  • Least privilege – Permission + Rights = Privilege

  • Employment & termination procedures – Secure onboarding & offboarding

  • Training & awareness for users – “See something – Say something”

  • Auditing requirements & frequency – Verification & Validation
