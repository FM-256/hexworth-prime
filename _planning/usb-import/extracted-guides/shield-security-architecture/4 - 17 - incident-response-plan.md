Incident response plan

 What are the Incident Response roles that NIST suggests? -

 NIST SP 800-61 R2 "Computer Security Incident Handling Guide" identifies the
 following groups as necessary when responding to an incident:

    • Management
    • Information Assurance
    • IT Support
    • Legal Department
    • Public Affairs & Media Relations
    • Human Resources
    • Business Continuity Planning
    • Physical Security & Facilities Management


 What does NIST say about Incident Response reports? -

 NIST SP 800-84 "Guide to Test, Training, and Exercise Programs for IT Plans and
 Capabilities" includes an after action report (AAR) template & includes the
 following sections:

    • Introduction/Description
    • Scope of the exercise
    • Objectives of the exercise
    • Description of the scenario
    • Findings, including observations/results & recommendations
