## Testing plans


### Objectives:

At the end of this episode, I will be able to:

Explain the importance of business continuity and disaster recovery concepts.

### External Resources:

Testing plans

 What is the role of compliance & leadership in BCDR testing? -

 Creating, implementing, & testing BCDR capabilities is often a requirement due
 to legal or regulatory oversight

 Senior leadership's participation & sponsorship of BCDR activities is key

 BCDR activities must be included in strategic objectives

 Metrics should be developed to quantify operational success


 How do we conduct BCDR plan testing? -

 Checklist test - copies of the BCDR plan are distributed to all the departments,
 teams, & other participants included in the plans so that they can review them
 & offer feedback regarding any required updates or changes

  ** Person or group responsible for maintaining the BCDR plans incorporates
  feedback & changes into a newly revised copy


 Walkthrough test - all groups included in the plan identify a representative to
 participate in a meeting to review the plans & analyze their effectiveness
 against various BCDR scenarios


 Tabletop exercise (TTX) - designed to evaluate the procedures in place for
 responding to an incident

  Will identify a specific objective or goal & use it to determine whether all
  parties involved in the response know what to do & how to work together

  Led by a person or group that will describe an imaginary event to which the
  team must respond


 Parallel test - DR site is isolated from the primary site & then activated

  Departments within the organization connect to the services running at the DR
  site to test their functionality

  Once the test is completed, the DR site is taken offline & normal connectivity
  is restored

  Does not impact live production systems; normal operations within the
  organization can continue in parallel to the DR test

  Reduces risks associated with DR site testing; no real data is directly impacted


 Full Interruption test - fail-over/fail-back exercise is performed against
 production systems & data

  Normal operations are performed from the DR site for a set duration before
  systems are restored to the primary site

  Clearly demonstrates the effectiveness of the BCDR plan

  Impacts normal operations while services are moved between sites
