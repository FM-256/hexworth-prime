### External Resources:

Deperimeterization/Zero Trust

What is Deperimeterization? -

 The deconstruction of the barrier(s) between what is considered to be
inside & outside :

 • Cloud
 • Remote Work
 • Mobile
 • Outsourcing & Contracting
 • Wi-Fi


What are Zero Trust Architecture (ZTA) techniques? -

 Everything is considered as external & designs adopt the adage of "never trust,
always verify" & "assume breach“, forcing every request & connection to be
explicitly evaluated & validated to ensure that it is fully authenticated,
authorized, & encrypted before proceeding

NIST SP 800-207 "Zero Trust Architecture" -  "cybersecurity paradigms that move
defenses from static, network-based perimeters to focus on users, assets,
& resources"

*** Microsegmentation plays a critical part in providing this level of isolation

NIST SP 800-207 is available here:
