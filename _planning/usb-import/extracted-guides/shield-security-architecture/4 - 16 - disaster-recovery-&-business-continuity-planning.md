 Recovery Site Strategies -

 Cold site:

    • has electricity but no computer equipment
    • lowest operating expense
    • takes weeks to activate

 Warm site:

    • datacenter capable of running critical systems
    • systems are pre-configured & mostly ready to operate, but re-configuration
    & preparation is needed in order for them to be ready to operate in place of
    the primary site
    • activation takes hours to a few days

 Hot site:

    • can be activated & used within minutes
    • the most expensive & complicated option to implement
    • little to no service disruption

 Mobile site:

    • a data center in a box
    • acquired via agreement with a mobile site operator
    • deliver & then configure as necessary
    • between a cold & warm site, with moderate costs & activation times
    spanning days to weeks

 Cloud:

     • use of public cloud services as a DR site; DR as a service (DRaaS)
     • cloud service is configured like a warm site
     • systems are pre-configured &/or data is replicated to the cloud platform
     in near real-time
     • services are activated on the cloud platform until they can be
     transitioned back to the primary site
