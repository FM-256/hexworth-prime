 What is Federation? -

 Enabling the portability of identity information across otherwise autonomous
 security domains:

 1. The user (principal) attempts to access a service provider (SP) or the
 relying party (RP). The service provider redirects the principal to the identity
 provider (IdP) to authenticate.

 2. The principal authenticates with the identity provider & obtains an
 attestation of identity, in the form of some sort of token or document signed
 by the IdP.

 3. The principal presents the attestation to the service provider. The SP can
 validate that the IdP has signed the attestation because of its trust
 relationship with the IdP.

 4. The service provider can now connect the authenticated principal to its own
 accounts database.

        • It may be able to query attributes of the user account profile held by
        the IdP, if the principal has authorized this type of access.
