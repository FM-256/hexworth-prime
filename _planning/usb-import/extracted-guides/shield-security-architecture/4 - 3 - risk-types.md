### External Resources:

Risk types

 What other types of Risk exist? -

 • Inherent Risk - Level of risk that exists BEFORE
 countermeasures/controls/safeguards have been applied

 • Residual Risk – Level of risk left over AFTER all
 countermeasures/controls/safeguards have been applied

 • Risk Appetite – Strategic measure of tolerable residual risk levels

 • Risk Exceptions – Method of addressing risk when ALL OTHER methods prove
 unacceptable

    Exceptions need to be documented, justified, and approved by senior
    leadership, including signatures
