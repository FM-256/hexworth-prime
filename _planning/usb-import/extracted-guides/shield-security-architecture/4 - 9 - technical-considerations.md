## Technical considerations


### Objectives:

At the end of this episode, I will be able to:

Explain the importance of managing and mitigating vendor risk.

### External Resources:

Technical considerations

 Technical Testing and Evaluation -

 Capabilities should be validated to ensure the service or product performs as
 required & supports the features & functionality expected

 Security capabilities should be validated to ensure the service or product
 operates safely & according to pre-established security requirements

 Documentation of all evaluation work & findings is necessary


 Network Segmentation:

    • Logically via Virtual Local Area Networks (VLAN)
    • Physically via air gapping
    • Designs should be periodically re-evaluated


Transmission Control:

    • How communication channels are protected
    • Access Control Lists (ACL) & rules
    • Mutual authentication & encryption


 Shared Credentials:

    • Maintain a one-to-one relationship between employees & credentials in
    order to establish clear accountability & as an effective means to revoke
    individual access

    • Credentials associated with software & services should be provisioned in
    a way that prevents them from being used as a standard account
