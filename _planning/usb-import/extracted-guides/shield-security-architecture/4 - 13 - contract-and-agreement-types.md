Contract and agreement types

 What are the contract & agreement types you need to know? -

 Master Service Agreement (MSA) - "umbrella" contracts establishing an agreement
 between two entities to conduct business during a defined term (typically
 a year); each engagement within the agreement is defined by individual scopes
 of work for expectations & deliverables

 Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA) - defines the conditions under which an entity
 cannot disclose, or share, information & includes specific descriptions of the
 penalties for breaking the agreement

 Memorandum of Understanding (MOU) - considered to be a non-binding agreement;
 a formal means to define roles and expectations

 Interconnection Security Agreement (ISA) - established when data needs to be
 shared; describing & defining the operating parameters, roles, requirements,
 & expectations of use

 Service Level Agreement (SLA) - the detailed terms under which a service is
 provided; includes financial penalties for non-compliance

 Operational-Level Agreement (OLA) – internal facing SLA; negotiated between
 business units

 Privacy Level Agreement (PLA) - used when establishing a relationship with a
 cloud service provider (CSP); metrics & measures related to conforming with
 specific information privacy & data protection requirements
