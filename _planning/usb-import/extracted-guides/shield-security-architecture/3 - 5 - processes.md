Processes

 What are Software Patching & Host Protection Processes? -

 Individual or team responsible for keeping up to date on security patch bulletins

 Mechanism(s) to patch operating systems & all applications

 Extending patch management principles to cloud resources

 Reviews & triages of updates into urgent, important, & non-critical categories

 Test environment where patches can be installed & tested for functionality &
 impact prior to deployment into production

 Detailed logs of patching activity that can be used to evaluate & monitor the
 deployment of patches

 Mechanism to evaluate & test firmware updates

 Ability to have an immediate administrative push delivery of approved urgent
 patches

 Periodic evaluation & full rollout for non-critical patches
