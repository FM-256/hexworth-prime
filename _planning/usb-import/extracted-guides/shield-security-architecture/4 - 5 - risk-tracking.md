Risk tracking

 How do we track risk? -

 Key Performance Indicators (KPI) -- Measure performance of a program against
 desired goals. Identify effectiveness of a cybersecurity program by defining
 the crucial goals and desired outcomes of the program.

  • Patch status, Compliance status, Time to resolve, etc…

  • Measure the scalability, reliability, and availability of an organization's
  critical infrastructure and services


 Key Risk Indicators (KRI)  -- Closely related to KPI’s. Analysis of KPI’s may
 expose trends that are indicative of additional risk items

  • Risk Indicator Chart


 Risk Register -- Identified in the ISO 27001 standard. Provides visualization
 of identified risks and includes descriptions and information about mitigating
 controls

    • The most visible output of the risk management program

    • The creation of the risk register requires collaboration between many
    departments and should be considered a dynamic work product, one that is
    never truly finished
