Legal Considerations

 What are the Legal Considerations you need to know? -

 Legal Jurisdiction - a particular geographic area containing a defined legal
 authority

 Due Care - a baseline that can be used to determine if reasonable safeguards
 are in place (how we act)

 Due Diligence - ongoing & documented effort to continuously evaluate & improve
 the mechanisms by which assets are protected (oversight)

 Legal/Litigation Hold - notification to preserve electronically stored
 information (ESI) &/or paper documents that may be relevant to a legal proceeding

 eDiscovery - the electronic component of identifying, collecting, & providing
 the electronically stored information (ESI) identified by a legal hold

 Export Controls
 Wassenaar Arrangement (1996) - conventional arms & dual-use goods & technologies

 Encryption Laws – vary widely
