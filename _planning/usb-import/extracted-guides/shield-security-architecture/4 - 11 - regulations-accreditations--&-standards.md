 What are the different types of Regulations, Accreditations & Standards? -

 General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) - rules for organizations that offer
 services to entities in the European Union (EU) or that collect &/or analyze
 data on a subject located there

 Rules apply no matter where the originating organization operates from or where
 the collected data is stored;  7 principles:

    • Lawfulness, fairness & transparency
    • Purpose limitation
    • Data minimization
    • Accuracy
    • Storage limitation
    • Integrity & confidentiality (security)
    • Accountability


 Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) - US federal law designed to
 protect the privacy of children (inside & outside of the Unites States) under
 the age of thirteen

    • Requires operators of websites or online services to provide clear privacy
    policies, details when consent from a parent or guardian is required

    • Describes the operator's responsibilities to protect information from
    being used for marketing purposes


 Payment Card Industry Data Security Standard (PCI DSS) - global data protection
 standard via a consortium of payment card companies

    • Identifies controls designed to prevent fraud & protect credit & debit card
    data

    • Organizations that take credit & debit cards are required to follow the
    standards described within the PCI DSS

    Access the standard here:
    https://www.pcisecuritystandards.org/document_library


 Capability Maturity Model Integration (CMMI) - describes five levels of maturity
 within the operations of an organization

    Level 1: Initial - Processes do not exist & work is reactive in nature

    Level 2: Managed - Many work activities are defined via processes but work
    is still frequently reactive in nature

    Level 3: Defined - The majority of work is well-defined via processes &
    proactive measures are in place

    Level 4: Quantitatively Managed - All work is well-defined via processes,
    proactive measures are in place, & the work outputs are tracked and analyzed

    Level 5: Optimizing - Work is well-defined via processes, work is proactive,
    measured, analyzed, & continuously improved


 The Security, Trust & Assurance registry (CSA STAR) - CSA STAR evaluation
 measures the security capabilities and privacy controls of a cloud service
 provider against the CSA Cloud Controls Matrix (CCM)

   CSA STAR assessments are available here:
   https://cloudsecurityalliance.org/star

   The CCM is available here:
   https://cloudsecurityalliance.org/research/cloud-controls-matrix/


 Protecting Privacy Data -

 The type of data that can uniquely identify an individual person (PII)

 Privacy is all about the capabilities of an individual to maintain control of
 information

 Different countries = different approaches

    United States - Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act (HIPAA),
    Gramm-Leach Bliley Act (GLBA), Children's Online Privacy Protection Act
    (COPPA), & California Consumer Protection Act (CCPA)

    The European Union's General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), Canada's
    Personal Information Protection & Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA), Japan's
    Act on the Protection of Personal Information (APPI), & the Personal Data
    Protection Act (PDPA) of Singapore


 What is Certification & Accreditation? -

 Certification –  documents claims that a system has been successfully built
 according to its requirement & that it is ready to be used

 Accreditation – documents the system owner's agreement & acceptance of
 certification

 Certification & Accreditation (C&A) includes four distinct phases:

  1. Initiation and Planning
  2. Certification
  3. Accreditation
  4. Continuous Monitoring


  Initiation and Planning - system owner & the designated Information System
  Security Officer (ISSO) identify & formally acknowledge that certification and
  accreditation is needed for a particular system

      • The initiation and planning phase requires the creation of a team to
      perform C&A activities, creating a formal project plan, & documenting the
      security classification of the system


 Certification - an independent audit will review the information system &
 associated documentation in order to identify if the necessary controls outlined
 in NIST special publication (SP) 800-53 have been implemented


 Accreditation - the Certifying Authority will also review the information
 system & the results of the independent audit

      • After the Certifying Authority reviews all necessary elements of the
      system and determines that the system is compliant with all requirements,
      a formal letter of accreditation will be provided to the system owner that
      grants the Authority to Operate (ATO) the system for a period of time
      (usually three years)


 Continuous Monitoring - the actions taken to ensure that a system continues to
 operate in a compliant way

    • The information system security officer (ISSO) has primary responsibility
    for this


   Often includes measurements & metrics detailed in the ISO standard 15408 -
   Common Criteria (CC) for Information Technology Security Evaluation

   Allows the security attributes of a system to be specifically detailed using
   a common vernacular

   The Common Criteria Portal can be accessed here:
   https://commoncriteriaportal.org/index.cfm
