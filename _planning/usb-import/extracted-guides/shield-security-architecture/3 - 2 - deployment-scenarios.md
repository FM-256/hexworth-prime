Deployment scenarios

 What are Mobile Device Deployment options? -

 The way(s) employees are provided with mobile devices & applications

    Bring your own device (BYOD) - device is owned by the employee & has to meet
    whatever profile is required by the company

    Corporate owned, business only (COBO) - device is property of the company &
    may only be used for company business

    Corporate owned, personally enabled (COPE) - device is chosen & supplied by
    the company & remains its property; employee may use it to access email &
    social media accounts & for personal web browsing but subject to whatever
    acceptable use policies are in force

    Choose your own device (CYOD) - same as COPE but the employee is given a
    choice of device from a list
