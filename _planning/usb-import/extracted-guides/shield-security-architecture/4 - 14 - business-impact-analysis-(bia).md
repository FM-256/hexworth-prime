## Business impact analysis (BIA)


### Objectives:

### External Resources:

Business impact analysis (BIA)

 What is a Business Continuity Plan (BCP)? -

 Focuses on how to continue operating during an unplanned disruption in service

 More comprehensive than a Disaster Recovery Plan (DRP) - Key component is a DRP

 Contains contingencies for business processes, assets, human resources &
 business partners – every aspect of the business that might be affected

 Manual workarounds should be outlined in the plan, so operations can continue
 until computer systems can be restored


 What are the 3 primary aspects of Business Continuity? -

 High availability - Capability & processes so that a business has access to
 applications regardless of local failures

 Continuous operations - Ability to keep things running during a disruption, as
 well as during planned outages such as scheduled backups or maintenance

 Disaster recovery - Recover a data center at a different site if a disaster
 destroys the primary site or otherwise renders it inoperable


 What does NIST SP 800-34 R1 have to say? -

 The Contingency Planning Guide for Federal Information Systems identifies the
 following steps for continuity planning:

  1. Develop the continuity planning policy statement
  2. Conduct the business impact analysis
  3. Identify preventive measures
  4. Create contingency strategies
  5. Develop an information system contingency plan
  6. Ensure plan testing, training, & exercises
  7. Ensure plan maintenance  


 What is the Business Impact Analysis (BIA)? -

 Predicts consequences of disruption of a business function & process & gathers
 information needed to develop recovery strategies

 Purpose is to correlate the system with the critical  mission/business processes
 & services provided, & based on that information, characterize the consequences
 of a disruption

 Potential loss scenarios should be identified during a risk assessment


 What are the key steps necessary to perform a BIA? -

 Determine mission/business processes & recovery criticality - Processes
 supported by the system are identified & the impact of a system disruption to
 those processes is determined along with outage impacts & estimated downtime

  ** Downtime should reflect the maximum time that an organization can tolerate
  while still maintaining the mission


 Identify resource requirements - Realistic recovery efforts require a thorough
 evaluation of the resources required to resume mission/business processes &
 related interdependencies as quickly as possible


 Identify recovery priorities for system resources - Based upon the results from
 the previous activities, system resources can be linked more clearly to
 critical mission/business processes & functions

  ** Priority levels can be established for sequencing recovery activities &
  resources


  What are recovery objectives? -

  Recovery Time Objective (RTO) - maximum amount of time that performing a
  recovery can take; Amount of system downtime the organization can withstand

 Recovery Point Objective (RPO) - amount of data that can be lost during a
 recovery without causing harm to the stability of the business

 Recovery Service Level (RSL) - documenting the services required & the expected
 level of service
