## Registration, proofing, and establishment of identity     

This process begins when a user is to be given their identity with the organization. 

NIST SP 800-63A provides guidance in this area. 

The process can begin with an applicant providing core attributes and evidence that is collected by the organization. This is the RESOLUTION phase of the process. The organization can then uniquely distinguish the individual among a given population or context. 

The next phase is VALIDATION. Here the evidence provided is validated. Here the authenticity, validity, and accuracy of the identity information can be determined and related to the real-life subject. 

The final phase is VERIFICATION. Here the evidence collected is verified and the linkage between the claimed identity and real-life existence of the subject presenting evidence can be confirmed and established. 

At this point, organizations can collect additional information that might be needed in the IAM process of the organization. For example, if biometric are used (let's say fingerprinting), this information can now be collected.
