# 1-6-1: Conduct Incident Management

After completing this episode, you should be able to:

+ Identify and explain incident management processes, given a scenario.

**Description:** In this episode, the learner will examine incident management, including response, the process, and industry resources for implementing incident management.

+ Describe the incident response process
  + Detection
    + Identify and confirm security incidents through continuous monitoring and analysis of systems and network traffic.
    + Utilize intrusion detection systems (IDS), security information and event management (SIEM) systems, and endpoint detection and response (EDR) tools.
    + Train staff to recognize signs of security incidents and report them promptly.
  + Response
    + Execute a coordinated strategy to contain and neutralize threats immediately upon detection.
    + Assemble an incident response team to tackle different aspects of the incident, including technical analysis, communication, and legal considerations.
    + Isolate affected systems to prevent the spread of the threat while maintaining essential business operations.
  + Mitigation
    + Apply measures to reduce the impact of an incident and prevent further damage to organizational assets.
    + Patch vulnerabilities, apply security updates, and change passwords to cut off unauthorized access.
    + Implement temporary controls if permanent solutions are not immediately available.
  + Reporting
    + Document the incident details, response actions, and outcomes for accountability and regulatory compliance.
    + Report the incident to relevant stakeholders, including management, legal authorities, and potentially affected clients.
    + Maintain clear and comprehensive logs of all incident-related activities for future reference and analysis.
  + Recovery
    + Restore affected systems and services to operational status, ensuring all systems are clean and secure.
    + Test restored systems to ensure they are functioning correctly and no longer compromised.
    + Gradually reintroduce systems back into the production environment, monitoring closely for any signs of recurrence.
  + Remediation
    + Analyze the root cause and exploit vectors of the incident; implement improvements to prevent future occurrences.
    + Update security policies, procedures, and technologies based on the findings from the incident analysis.
    + Strengthen defenses by addressing identified vulnerabilities and improving security awareness among employees.
  + Lessons learned
    + Conduct a post-incident review to evaluate the response effectiveness and incorporate learnings into future security strategies.
    + Share knowledge gained from the incident with all relevant personnel to improve organizational resilience.
    + Update the incident response plan based on lessons learned to better prepare for future incidents.
+ Describe industry resources for standards, frameworks and guidelines
  + NIST SP 800-61
    + Computer Security Incident Handling Guide
      + Provides guidelines for establishing an effective incident response program, including preparation, detection and analysis, containment, eradication, and recovery.
  + ISO/IEC 27035
    + Information Security Incident Management
      + An international standard providing a structured and planned approach to detecting, reporting, assessing, responding to, and learning from information security incidents.
  + SANS Institute Incident Handler's Handbook
    + Offers a high-level overview of the processes and steps involved in handling an incident effectively, from preparation to recovery and post-incident activity.
  + FIRST \(Forum of Incident Response and Security Teams\) Standards
    + Provides best practices, tools, and guidelines designed to help organizations prepare for and respond to incidents effectively.
  + CERT-RMM \(CERT Resilience Management Model\)
    + A capability model for managing operational resilience, including security incident management processes.
  + COBIT 5 \(Control Objectives for Information and Related Technologies\)
    + Framework for IT management and governance that includes processes and controls for managing IT incidents, including security incidents.
