Other Cryptanalytic Attacks

*3.7 Understand Methods of Cryptanalytic Attacks*

Description
--------------------------
In this episode, we will take a look at a bevy of other cyrptanalytic attacks such as
Man-in-the-Middle, Meet-in-the-Middle, Pass-the-Hash, Kerberos, and Ransomware.

Notes
--------------------------
+ Man-in-the-Middle
+ Meet-in-the-Middle

    + Attacker encrypts plain-text with every possible key
    + Attacker decrypts ciphertext with every possible key
      - When match is found, corresponding key pair represents both encrypt/decrypt capabilities
