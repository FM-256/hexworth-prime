Secure Site And Facility Design Basics
=======================================================

*3.8 Apply Security Principles to Site and Facility Design*
-------------------------------------------------------

Description
--------------------------
In this episode, we will look into designing security for our physical sites and 
facilities as well as why it's important for not just data protection, but also
human protection.  


Resources
--------------------------
+ https://www.cpted.net/

Learning Objectives
--------------------------
+ Explain why secure site and facility design is important
+ Explain how the Layered Defense Model applies to secure site and facility design
+ Explain how CPTED applies to secure site and facility design


Notes
--------------------------
+ Why is Secure Site/Facility Design important?
  - Human health and saftey (MOST IMPORTANT)
  - Protect property, assets, and operations using **Administrative**, **Physical**, and **Technical** controls
    + Mitigate or elimitate possible/likely threats
      - Both External and Internal threats
        + Espionage
        + Sabotage
    + Regulatory Compliance
    + BCDR
      - More robust against disaster
        + Could mitigate disaster entirely
        + Faster recovery in the event disaster occurs
    + Trust and Reputation
      - Stakeholders, Partners, Clients more likely to buy-in when security is an obvious priority
+ What should be the focus and functional order of our secure design?
  - Deter
    + Visible security measures deter malicious activity
  - Deny
  - Detect
  - Delay
+ Layered Defenses (Defense-in-Depth)
  - Multi-tiered defense controls
    + Complimentary security controls
      - One control responds from another
        + If outer doors are breached, inner doors auto-lock
+ Crime Prevention Through Environmental Design (CPTED)
  - Site/Community design principles with crime prevention in mind 
  - View web resource 
+ Equipment Failure Metrics
  - **Service Level Agreements** (SLA)
    + Acceptable level of downtime
  - **Mean Time to Failure** (MTTF)
    + Average lifespan of a NON-REPARIABLE asset
  - **Mean Time Between Failures** (MTBF)
    + Average time it takes for a REPARIABLE asset to fail again
  - **Mean Time to Repair** (MTTR)
    + Average time it takes to troubleshoot and repair failed equipment
