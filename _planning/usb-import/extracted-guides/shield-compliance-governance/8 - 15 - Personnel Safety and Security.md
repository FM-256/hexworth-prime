# 1-15-1: Personnel Safety and Security

After completing this episode, you should be able to:

+ Identify and explain personnel safety and security techniques, given a scenario

**Description:** In this episode, the learner will examine personnel safety and security concepts and considerations. We will explore various industry resources for guidance on protecting the safety and security of an organization's personnel.




+ Describe personnel safety and security concerns
  + Travel
    + Strategies and measures to ensure employee safety and data protection when traveling on business, addressing risks from physical threats to information security.
    + Risks
      + Data Theft
        + Risk of sensitive corporate information or personal data being stolen, especially through lost or stolen devices like laptops and smartphones.
     + Unsecured Networks
      + Using unsecured Wi-Fi networks at hotels, airports, or cafes, which cybercriminals can exploit to intercept data.
     + Physical Device Security
      + Devices being lost, stolen, or accessed by unauthorized individuals, leading to potential data breaches.
    + Social Engineering Attacks
      + Employees are more susceptible to phishing and other social engineering attacks while in unfamiliar environments.
    + Intellectual Property Exposure
      + Accidental or coerced disclosure of trade secrets or proprietary information during interactions with outsiders.
    + Compliance Risks
      + Failing to adhere to data protection regulations specific to different regions, potentially resulting in legal and financial repercussions.
    + Malware Infection
      + Increased risk of devices being infected with malware or ransomware through malicious software or hardware encountered while traveling.
    + Emergency Management
    + Coordinated efforts to prepare for, respond to, and recover from emergencies, aiming to protect people, assets, and operations from adverse events.
  + Duress
    + Situations where individuals are compelled to act against their ethical beliefs or organizational policies under undue pressure or threats, particularly from authoritative figures within the organization.
+ Describe how training provides increased security
  + Security Training and Awareness
    + Educational efforts to equip employees with knowledge and practices to safeguard organizational assets, emphasizing proactive behavior against potential threats.
+ Describe some risks associated with personnel security
    + Insider Threat
      + The risk posed by individuals within the organization who might exploit their access to sensitive information or systems to cause harm.
    + Social Media Impacts
      + Training employees on the potential security risks associated with social media use, including unintentional sharing of confidential information and susceptibility to phishing or social engineering attacks.
    + Two-Factor Authentication \(2FA\) Fatigue
      + The phenomenon where constant security authentication demands lead to user negligence or shortcuts, potentially compromising security protocols.
