# 1-11-1: Implement Disaster Recovery Processes

After completing this episode, you should be able to:

+ Identify and explain disaster recovery processes, given a scenario

**Description:** In this episode, the learner will examine disaster recovery processes. We will explore the steps, industry resources for disaster recovery strategies, guidance, and more.

+ Describe a Disaster Recovery Plan \(DRP\)
  + Provides the procedures for relocating information systems operations to an alternate location
+ Describe disaster recovery \(DR\) activities
  + Response
    + Activate the disaster recovery plan immediately following the detection of a disaster.
    + Implement initial containment strategies to limit damage, such as isolating affected systems or shutting down vulnerable services.
    + Mobilize the disaster recovery team and begin executing pre-defined response actions based on the type and severity of the disaster.
  + Personnel
    + Clearly outline disaster recovery roles and responsibilities in the DR plan.
    + Establish a chain of command and ensure key personnel, such as DR coordinators and IT specialists, are accessible and ready to act.
    + Set up an alternate workforce plan for scenarios where primary staff are unavailable.
    + NIST 800-34
      + Occupant Emergency Plan \(OEP\) - provides coordinated process for minimizing loss of life or injury and to protect property damage in response to a physical threat
  + Communications
    + Establish predefined communication protocols and methods, including backup systems, to maintain contact among DR team members, employees, stakeholders, and customers.
    + NIST SP 800-34 - Crisis Communication Plan \(CCP\) - provides procedures for cirulating communications internally and externally; means to provide critical status information and controls rumors.
    + Provide regular updates on the disaster status, recovery efforts, and expected timelines for resolution.
    + Secure and use alternate communication methods if primary channels are compromised.
  + Assessment
    + Conduct an immediate evaluation of the disaster's scope, including which services, systems, and assets are affected.
    + Assess the impact on business operations, data integrity, and overall organizational functionality.
    + Prioritize actions based on the criticality of impacted assets and operations to the business.
  + Restoration
    + Implement steps to recover and restore critical IT systems, applications, and data from backups to a pre-disaster state.
    + Use predefined criteria to determine the order of service restoration, focusing first on the most critical services that support essential business functions.
    + Test restored systems and services to ensure they are functioning correctly before bringing them back online.
  + Training and awareness
    + Testing, Training & Exercises \(TT&E\)
    + Develop and deliver ongoing disaster recovery training and simulation exercises to ensure personnel are familiar with DR procedures and their specific roles.
    + Raise awareness about disaster recovery protocols among all employees, not just the DR team, to foster a culture of preparedness.
    + Update training materials and simulations regularly to reflect changes in the DR plan, technology, and personnel.
    + NIST SP 800-84 Guide to Test, Training, and Exercise Programs for IT Plans and Capabilities
  + Lessons learned
    + After a disaster event, conduct a thorough review and debriefing to identify what was successful and what areas need improvement.
    + Document all findings, decisions, and actions taken during the disaster for future reference and learning.
    + Update the disaster recovery plan after performing lessons learned to strengthen future response and recovery efforts, ensuring the plan remains effective and current.
+ Describe available industry resources for guidance on DR
  + NIST Documentation
    + NIST SP 800-34 Rev. 1: "Contingency Planning Guide for Federal Information Systems." This publication specifically addresses disaster recovery as part of a comprehensive approach to contingency planning.
    + NIST SP 800-53 - Security and Privacy Controls for Information Systems and Organizations
  + ISO/IEC Documentation:
    + ISO/IEC 27031: "Information technology — Security techniques — Guidelines for information and communication technology readiness for business continuity." This standard provides guidance on the ICT aspects of business continuity, including disaster recovery.
    + ISO 22301: "Security and resilience — Business continuity management systems — Requirements." While this standard covers broader business continuity management, it includes requirements that impact disaster recovery planning and execution.
