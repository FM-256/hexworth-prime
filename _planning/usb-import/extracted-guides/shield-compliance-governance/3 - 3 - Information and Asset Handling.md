After completing this episode, you should be able to:

+ Establish information and asset handling requirements

**Description:** In this episode, you will learn key concepts associated with establishing information and asset handling requirements in a corporate setting.

## Establish Information and Asset Handling Requirements 

Your organization should establish strict rules over how assets (including data) area handled day to day. 

Special attention should be given to these categories: 

+ Media - these are the components used to physically store the data; data must be encrypted on the media in order to ensure confidentiality; technical, physical, logical, and administrative controls applied as appropriate   
+ Marking - all media and even some data itself should be marked with a label indicating classification 
+ Handling - you should design and define policies for the handling of the various asset classifications; these policies should be communicated and then trained on 
+ Storing - data that is stored should be encrypted as required 
+ Destruction - this actually covers destruction and disposal and will be elaborated on later 

Retention periods are a great example of a policy we must create, educate on, and enforce. These are going to vary for the different classifications of data. 

>**Note:** Being able to design proper information handling requirements hinges on the strength of our data inventory procedures. This should follow a process similar to scanning for data; classifying the data; labeling the data; and reporting on the data.
