## Account Access Review         

Auditing the effectiveness of access controls is a typical step in the overall management of identity lifecycles. 

Notice that auditing has a dual meaning. There is the auditing process that creates audit logs, and then there is the process of auditing access controls. This is a process that involves individuals selected to perform the audit. 

Account access reviews will often:

+ Verify that accounts do not have excessive privileges 
+ Verify that accounts are managed and used properly 
+ Ensure that secure processes and procedures are in place 
+ Ensure that personnel are following procedures 
+ Enure that policies and procedures are functioning as designed 
+ Ensure accounts are disabled and deleted as required
