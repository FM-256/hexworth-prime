## Standards Selection When Determining Security Controls    

Are you required to comply with specific security standards as part of your business and public regulations? 

Do you want to use celebrated standards to help develop your own standards? 

Important standards bodies to know: 

+ International Organization for Standardization (ISO) - international body consisting of members from multiple national bodies; principle information security standards - ISO 27000; ISO/IEC 27001 ISMS
+ National Institute of Standards and Technology (NIST) - non-regulatory agency of the United States Department of Commerce; promote innovation and industrial competitiveness; SP 800 (Computer Security) - SP 1800 (Cybersecurity practice guides) - SP 500 (Information technology) - NIST Cybersecurity Framework - 800-30 (Guide for conducting risk assessments) 
+ British Standards Institution (BSI) - the national standards body of the UK; ISO/IEC 27001 
+ Internet Engineering Task Force (IETF) - open standards organization; standards with TCP/IP; organized into working groups 
+ Payment Card Industry Security Standards Council (PCI SSC) - PCI SSC is a global, open body responsible for creating, improving, disseminating and helping with the understanding of the security standards for payment account security; Payment Card Industry Data Security Standard (PCI DSS); Payment Application Data Security Standard (PA-DSS)
