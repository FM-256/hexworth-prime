## Some Data Protection Methods     

Digital Rights Management (DRM) - a technology that helps control and protect digital content like music, movies, or software. It aims to prevent unauthorized copying and distribution by restricting access to the content. DRM uses encryption and access controls to limit how and where a digital file can be used, ensuring that only people who have the proper permissions or licenses can access and use the content. While it helps content creators and distributors protect their intellectual property, DRM has been a subject of debate due to its potential impact on user privacy and the ability to make fair use of digital media.

Data Loss Prevention (DLP) - a set of strategies and tools designed to safeguard sensitive information within organizations and prevent it from being accidentally or intentionally shared, accessed, or leaked. It involves identifying and classifying sensitive data, monitoring its flow across networks, endpoints, and storage systems, and implementing controls to prevent unauthorized disclosure. DLP helps organizations avoid data breaches, comply with regulations, and protect confidential information like personal data, financial records, or intellectual property. By setting rules and policies, DLP solutions can detect and respond to potential data breaches, providing an extra layer of security against data loss.

Cloud Access Security Brokers (CASB) - are like guardians for your data when using cloud services. Imagine them as security checkpoints between your organization's internal network and the cloud applications your employees use. CASBs help ensure that sensitive data remains secure as it travels to and from the cloud. They act as intermediaries, monitoring and controlling how users access and share data in cloud platforms. CASBs can enforce security policies, encrypt data, and detect and respond to potential threats or policy violations, providing an extra layer of protection for your organization's information in the cloud. In essence, CASBs help maintain a secure bridge between your internal systems and the cloud services your business relies on.
