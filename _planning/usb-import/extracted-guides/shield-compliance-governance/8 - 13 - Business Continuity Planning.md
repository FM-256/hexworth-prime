# 1-13-1: Business Continuity Planning

After completing this episode, you should be able to:

+ Identify and explain the importance of business continuity processes, given a scenario 

**Description:** In this episode, the learner will examine contingency planning concepts and activities. We will explore various continuity planning types, terminology, industry resources for guidance, and more.

+ Describe contingency planning
  + Establishing "procedures for assessment and recovery of a system following a disruption
+ Describe the contingency planning process
  + Develop a Contingency Planning Policy Statement
    + A formal policy defining an organization's overall contingency objectives
    + Establishes organizational responsibilities and a framework for system contingency planning
  + Conduct the Business Impact Analysis \(BIA\)
    + The BIA identifies the critical information systems and processes and the potential impact of their disruption
    + This BIA helps prioritize resources and recovery efforts based on the severity of the impact
  + Identify Preventive Controls
    + Steps taken to reduce the adverse effects of system disruptions, increasing  system availability, reduce lifecycle costs
  + Develop Recovery Strategies 
    + Creating thorough recovery strategies, ensuring systems can be quickly restored to operational status after a disruption.
    + Strategies should address the recovery of both information technology \(IT\) infrastructure and operations
  + Develop an Information System Contingency Plan
    + The plan should include detailed guidance and procedures for restoring a damaged system, tailored to the system, its resources, and the BIA's backup requirements
  + Ensure Plan Testing, Training, and Exercises
    + Testing the plan identifies its weaknesses, and training prepares recovery personnel for plan activation
    + Also known as TT&E
    + Activities that can improve plan effectiveness and overall agency preparedness
  + Ensure Plan Maintenance
    + The plan should be a living document and updated regularly 
    + Adjust with system enhancements and organizational changes
    + This step ensures the contingency plan evolves in line with the system it protects
+ The BCP forms a cyclical process to ensure that contingency planning is integrated into the lifecycle of the system, making the system more resilient and prepared for unexpected disruptions.
+ Describe additional contingency planning concepts 
  + ISCP \(Information System Contingency Plan\) 
    + A comprehensive document that establishes procedures to recover an information system following a disruption or failure
    + The ISCP aims to restore the system to an operational state, ensuring the availability, integrity, and confidentiality of the system and its information
  + Business Continuity Plan \(BCP\) 
    + A strategic and operational plan that outlines how an organization will continue its essential functions in the event of various  emergencies or disruptions
    + The BCP focuses on sustaining an organization's business processes during and after a disaster.
  + Continuity of Operations Plan \(COOP\)
    + A plan developed by federal agencies to ensure the continuous performance of essential functions or operations during a wide range of emergencies   
    + Includes localized acts of nature, accidents, and technological or attack-related emergencies
  + Occupant Emergency Plan \(OEP\)
    + A plan that outlines the procedures for safeguarding lives and property within a specific facility or geographical area in the event of an emergency
    + The primary focus is on the safety and evacuation of personnel
  + Crisis Communications Plan \(CCP\) 
    + A component of contingency planning that focuses on the mechanisms, procedures, and systems for communicating with internal and external stakeholders
    + Including the media during and after an emergency
    + Ensures accurate and timely information is communicated during crises and controls rumors
