Utilizing Natural Access Controls
=======================================================

*3.8 Apply Security Principles to Site and Facility Design*
-------------------------------------------------------

Description
--------------------------
In this episode, we will investigate protecting our physical sites and facilities
using natural access controls.


Resources
--------------------------
+ https://www.cpted.net/Primer-in-CPTED


Learning Objectives
--------------------------
+ Describe Natural Access Controls
+ Describe Natural Surveillance
+ Describe Territorial Control


Notes
--------------------------
+ What is the idea behind Natural Access Controls
  - Specific to entrances 
    + Where they are
    + What's around them
    + Landscaping 
    + Lighting
  - Each entrance is a possible security zone
    + This is where the initial attack would come from 
      - So focus security around that zone
        + Ingress/Egress flow control
          - Special entrance consideration for suppliers
+ Natural Surveillance
  - Increase visibility of attack(ers) through strategic planning and use of the physical environmet
    + An empty building makes it easier for attackers to...
      - Enter the building unnoticed
      - Move through the building unnoticed
      - Setup and carry out attacks unnoticed
      - Leave the building unnoticed 
    + The more populated the building (strategically)
      - The more likely we are to deter, deny, detect, delay
+ Territorial Control
  - Fostering a sense of ownership and community in the people that occupy the site
    + Done using fences, lighting, landscape, etc
    + This can affect the anxiety level of potential attackers
      - Nervous outsiders are likely to...
        + Be noticed / call attention to themselves
        + Make mistakes
        + Not go through with malicious plans
