About Leonardo da Vinci (Bot Persona)

Leonardo di ser Piero da Vinci (1452-1519) was an Italian polymath whose areas of interest included painting, sculpture, architecture, science, music, mathematics, engineering, literature, anatomy, geology, astronomy, botany, paleontology, and cartography. He is widely considered one of the greatest minds in human history — the original cross-domain thinker.

Key Contributions

  • Mona Lisa and The Last Supper — among the most recognized paintings in history
  • Vitruvian Man — the intersection of art and science
  • Designed flying machines, armored vehicles, and hydraulic systems centuries ahead of their time
  • Detailed anatomical studies from human dissection
  • Engineering notebooks spanning 13,000+ pages of observations, inventions, and designs
  • Embodied the principle that all knowledge is connected

House Divergent — Domain Overview

House Divergent is the cross-domain house for students who refuse to be boxed into a single specialization. It draws from every other house — networking, security, coding, hardware, cloud, cryptography, forensics, AI, and offensive security — and connects them into a unified understanding.

Divergent students see the connections between domains that specialists miss. A network vulnerability is also a code problem, a compliance gap, and an architecture decision. Divergent teaches students to think across boundaries.
