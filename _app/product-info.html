Platform Capabilities

12 Learning Houses

Specialized tracks covering networking, security, cloud, forensics, ethical hacking, scripting, AI, hardware, web development, and more. 3,500+ interactive modules with terminal simulations, quizzes, and hands-on labs.

120+ CTF Boxes + The Dojo

Capture The Flag challenges from beginner to expert. 15 open-world investigations simulating corporate espionage, money laundering, and insider threats. Plus 120 vulnerability challenges across 5 belt levels in The Dojo.

The Operator — 120 Coding Missions

Metroidvania-style progression across 7 programming languages. Students earn permanent tools as they advance. Python, JavaScript, Bash, SQL, and more — the training room IS the mission.

The Case Room — Ethical Decision Training

Students investigate real-world cases, commit to decisions under uncertainty, and defend their reasoning against structured challenges from opposing ethical frameworks. 10 cases based on documented events. No right answers, only defensible ones. Evidence analysis, stakeholder mapping, framework collision, and professional code conflict resolution.

The Panopticon + Tournaments

Live streaming gallery where spectators watch every competitor in real-time. Team-based tournaments, VS head-to-head mode, co-op missions, and 2,000+ achievements drive engagement.

202 Portfolio Projects

100 software projects (AI, cloud, security, web, data) and 102 hardware projects (Signal Hub) — all GitHub-publishable. Students graduate with a verifiable portfolio, not just a transcript.

Server-Side Assessment

200+ assessments graded by Cloud Functions — answers never exist in the browser. Mapped to CompTIA, Cisco, Microsoft, and AWS certifications. The grade means something.

12 AI House Advisors

Every house has an embedded AI specialist — Turing, Mitnick, Lovelace, Cerf, Shannon, and more. Each carries the knowledge and temperament of its historical namesake to guide students.

Instructor Dashboard & Analytics

Automated gradebook, engagement analytics, struggle detection, class management with join codes, assignment sequencing, and CSV exports. Adjunct-friendly — deploy a full course in minutes.
