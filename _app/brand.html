05 · Usage

Using the mark

Do

  • Use the cyan or white mark on dark backgrounds
  • Use the black mark on light backgrounds
  • Keep clear space of at least the hexagon's height around it
  • Scale from the provided sizes to stay crisp

Don't

  • Recolor the mark outside the brand cyan
  • Stretch, rotate, or add effects to the mark
  • Place the neon emblem on a light background
  • Crowd the mark against text or edges
