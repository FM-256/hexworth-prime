Mission Brief

You are executing a historical WEP exploitation exercise against the WF-WEP-Legacy network in the WifiForge isolated lab environment. Use iperf to generate UDP traffic that floods the AP with data frames, collect the resulting Initialization Vectors (IVs), and crack the 64-bit WEP key using aircrack-ng's PTW statistical attack. This is the foundational WiFi attack that made WEP's deprecation inevitable.
