Mission Brief

This is the WifiForge orientation lab. Before attacking anything, you must know how to navigate the framework itself. Launch the WifiForge menu, verify the virtual network topology is running, inventory your wireless interfaces, confirm lab materials are in place, and select a lab to begin. Every subsequent lab assumes you can do this cold.
