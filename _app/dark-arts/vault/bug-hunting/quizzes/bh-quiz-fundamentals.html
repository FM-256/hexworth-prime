  • VDPs only accept reports from internal employees
  • BBPs require NDA agreements while VDPs do not
  • VDPs provide safe harbor for reporting but typically do not offer monetary rewards, while BBPs pay bounties

Q14 What is the purpose of certificate transparency (CT) logs in bug bounty reconnaissance?
  • They allow you to revoke SSL certificates for target domains
  • They decrypt HTTPS traffic for inspection
  • They provide a public record of issued certificates, revealing subdomains and internal hostnames that may not be in DNS
  • They verify that a website's TLS configuration is secure
Q15 A fellow researcher found bugs by "testing features that were recently deployed." What recon strategy aligns with this approach?
  • Scanning the entire application again with the same methodology
  • Monitoring for changes using tools like VisualPing, comparing Wayback snapshots, or watching changelog/release notes to identify new attack surface
  • Switching to a completely different program
  • Asking the researcher to share their exact findings so you can duplicate them
