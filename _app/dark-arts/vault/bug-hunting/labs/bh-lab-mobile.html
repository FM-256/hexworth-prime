Mission Brief

You are conducting an authorized mobile security assessment of the ShopZone Android application (com.shopzone.app). Your objective is to decompile the APK, analyze the source for hardcoded secrets and insecure storage, discover debug endpoints, and use Frida and Objection to perform runtime analysis, certificate pinning bypass, and credential extraction.

Legal Warning
Reverse engineering mobile applications may violate terms of service, the DMCA anti-circumvention provisions (17 U.S.C. 1201), and the CFAA. Runtime instrumentation with Frida requires a rooted/jailbroken test device and explicit authorization. This lab is fully simulated.
