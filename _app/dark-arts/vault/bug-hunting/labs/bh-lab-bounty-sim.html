Bug Bounty Simulation

You have been invited to NovaTech Corp's private bug bounty program. Your objective: discover vulnerabilities, document them professionally, and earn bounties based on severity and report quality.

This simulation covers the full bug bounty lifecycle -- from scope review through payout. Seven vulnerabilities are planted across NovaTech's attack surface.

Phase 1: Scope Review

Before hunting, review the program policy carefully. Understanding scope prevents wasted effort and policy violations.

NovaTech Corp Bug Bounty Program

Program ID: NT-BBP-2026 | Status: Active | Response SLA: 48h

In Scope

  • *.novatech.com (web applications)
  • api.novatech.com (REST API)
  • mobile.novatech.com (mobile web)
  • app.novatech.com (primary application)

Out of Scope

  • blog.novatech.com (third-party CMS)
  • status.novatech.com (monitoring page)
  • Physical / social engineering attacks
  • DoS / DDoS attacks
  • Automated scanning without prior approval

Reward Table

Critical$5,000 - $15,000
High$2,000 - $5,000
Medium$500 - $2,000
Low$100 - $500

Rules of Engagement

  • No automated scanning without prior approval
  • Do not access other users' data beyond proof-of-concept
  • Report within 24 hours of discovery
  • 90-day coordinated disclosure timeline
  • One report per vulnerability -- do not chain unrelated issues
  • Testing accounts available: testuser / testpass123
