What You Learned

  • Spoofed domains -- attackers use lookalike domains (micros0ft vs microsoft) to impersonate trusted senders
  • Urgency tactics -- creating panic to bypass critical thinking ("account suspended in 24 hours")
  • Mismatched URLs -- hover over links to reveal the actual destination before clicking
  • Grammar errors -- professional organizations proofread their communications
  • Brand inconsistencies -- subtle misspellings in logos and branding reveal fakes

Phishing remains the most common initial attack vector in cybersecurity incidents. Training users to spot these indicators is critical defense.
