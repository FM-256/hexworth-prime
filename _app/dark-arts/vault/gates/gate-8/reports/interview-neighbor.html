WITNESS INTERVIEW TRANSCRIPT

Case: 2026-MP-04871 | Interviewer: Det. P. Novak | Date: Feb 13, 2026, 1530hrs
Witness: James Chen
Address: 1847 Wilson Blvd, Apt 4B, Arlington, VA 22201
Relationship to Subject: Neighbor (adjacent unit, same floor)
Contact: 703-555-0182
How long have you known Mr. Reeves?
About two years, since he moved in. We're friendly — say hello in the hall, that sort of thing. He's quiet, keeps to himself mostly. Sometimes we'd grab coffee in the building lobby, talk about work stress, the usual neighbor stuff.
When did you last see him?
Monday evening, around 6:30. He was in the hallway, looked like he was heading out. Dressed nicer than usual — button-down shirt, dark slacks, nice shoes. Not his typical jeans and polo. I asked if he had a hot date and he laughed, said he was meeting someone for dinner.
Did he say who or where?
No, just "dinner with a colleague." He seemed in a good mood, maybe a little preoccupied. Like his mind was somewhere else, you know? But not upset or anything. Just... focused.
Did you see or hear him after that?
Not see, but hear. Tuesday — I think it was late afternoon, maybe 4 or 5 — I could hear him moving around through the wall. The walls aren't that thick between our units. It sounded like he was opening and closing drawers, moving things around. Systematic, like packing maybe? I remember thinking maybe he had a work trip coming up.
What about Wednesday morning?
I'm a light sleeper. I heard his front door close really early — I checked my clock, it was 5:07 AM. I looked out my window and saw a taxi idling outside the building entrance. Yellow cab, not Uber. I figured he was catching an early flight somewhere for work. He travels sometimes for his job.
When did you become concerned?
Thursday evening when I got home from work. I noticed his newspapers stacking up outside his door — he gets the Washington Post delivered every morning. There were three of them just sitting there. No lights in his place that I could see through the crack under the door. Friday morning I knocked a few times, no answer. I tried calling his cell phone, went straight to voicemail. That's when I called you guys.
Has he ever disappeared like this before?
He travels for work sometimes, but he usually mentions it. Like, "Hey James, I'll be in Athens next week" or whatever. This just felt... off. The newspapers piling up, the car still in the garage. If he drove somewhere, he would have taken the car. And leaving his phone? That's weird, right? Nobody goes anywhere without their phone these days.
Anything else unusual recently?
Now that you mention it — maybe a week ago, Tuesday or Wednesday, I saw a woman leaving his apartment. Early evening, maybe 6 PM. Professional looking, dark hair pulled back in a bun, maybe mid-40s. She was wearing a blazer and carrying a manila folder under her arm. Could have been work-related, I don't know. I'd never seen her before though, and Daniel doesn't exactly have a parade of visitors.
Can you describe her in more detail?
Average height, maybe 5'6" or 5'7". Fit, like she works out. The thing I remember most was how composed she looked. Not rushed, not flustered, just... in control. She walked to the elevator like she owned the place. Very deliberate.
Did you mention this to Mr. Reeves?
No, I didn't see him after that until Monday when he was heading out to dinner. And I wasn't going to be nosy — people have their privacy.
