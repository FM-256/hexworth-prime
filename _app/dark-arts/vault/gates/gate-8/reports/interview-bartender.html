WITNESS INTERVIEW TRANSCRIPT

Case: 2026-MP-04871 | Interviewer: Det. P. Novak | Date: Feb 13, 2026, 1700hrs
Witness: Maria Santos
Occupation: Bartender, The Anchor Restaurant
Location: 3108 M St NW, Georgetown, Washington DC 20007
Relationship to Subject: Service worker (no prior relationship)
Contact: 202-555-0293
Were you working Monday evening, February 10th?
Yes, I was on the evening shift from 5 PM to close. That's my regular Monday schedule.
Do you recognize this man? [Shows photo of Daniel Reeves]
Yeah, I think so. Monday night, he came in around 7, maybe a little after. Booth 12, in the back corner. We don't get as crowded on Mondays, so I remember him.
Was he alone?
No, a woman was already waiting for him when he arrived. She got there maybe 15 minutes before, asked for the back booth specifically. Ordered a Maker's Mark neat while she waited. Very put-together — dark hair pulled back in a bun, nice charcoal blazer. Not flashy, just... composed. Like she was used to being in charge of things.
Can you describe their interaction?
They spoke quietly. Really quietly — I noticed because most people in that booth are louder, it's kind of a date spot. These two were leaned in close, intense. Not romantic though. More like... business. Serious business. She did most of the talking at first, he mostly listened and nodded.
Did you see them exchange anything?
When I brought their second round — they both had bourbon, his was on the rocks — I noticed she had a folder on the table. Manila, nothing written on the outside. At one point she slid something across the table to him. A photograph, I think, or maybe a small document. He picked it up, studied it for a long time without saying anything, then put it in his inside jacket pocket.
How long did they stay?
Maybe an hour, hour and fifteen minutes total? They ordered appetizers and entrees. She left first — stood up, shook his hand very formally, took the folder with her. Professional handshake, not a hug or anything personal. He stayed another 15 minutes or so after she left, just sitting there nursing his drink. Looked like he was thinking hard about something.
How did he pay?
That was unusual actually. He split the payment — cash for the drinks, credit card for the food. Most people just put it all on one card. He specifically asked me to split it that way. Gave me two twenties for the drinks and said keep the change, then swiped his card for the meals.
Did you notice anything else about his behavior?
He checked his phone a couple times after she left. And when he was getting ready to leave, he looked around the room — not like he forgot something, more like he was checking if anyone was watching him. Paranoid might be too strong a word, but... aware. Very aware of his surroundings. Then he put on his jacket and left.
What about the woman — did she seem nervous or rushed?
No, complete opposite. Calm, methodical. She barely touched her food, but she wasn't anxious. Just... focused. Like she was there for a specific purpose and everything else was background noise.
Did you hear any of their conversation?
Just fragments when I was nearby. I heard her say something about "the timeline" and "operational security." And at one point she said "Istanbul" — I remember that because I've always wanted to visit Turkey. But I wasn't trying to eavesdrop, and they were keeping their voices down.
Would you recognize the woman if you saw her again?
Probably. She had a distinctive presence. And that folder — it had some kind of seal on it, like an official stamp or emblem. Dark blue ink. I only saw it for a second when she stood up.
