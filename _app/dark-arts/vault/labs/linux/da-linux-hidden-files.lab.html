Lab 1-3: Hidden Files & Ghost Artifacts

Mission Briefing

You have been given access to a server suspected of data exfiltration. Initial scans by the security team found nothing unusual -- the directory appears to contain only routine quarterly reports. Your mission: dig deeper. Attackers routinely hide evidence in dot-files and nested hidden directories that standard listing commands will not reveal. Find every artifact the attacker left behind.

MITRE ATT&CK Mapping
T1564 -- Hide Artifacts T1036 -- Masquerading
Forensic Tip
By default, ls does not show files or directories that begin with a dot (.). Attackers exploit this behavior to hide malicious tools, exfiltration logs, and C2 beacons in plain sight. Always use ls -a or find during an investigation to ensure nothing is missed.
