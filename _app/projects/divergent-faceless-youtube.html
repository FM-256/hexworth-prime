Subject

Build a Faceless YouTube Channel

Operator 180 min 1000 XP
Mission Brief

This operation covers the full architecture of a faceless YouTube channel built entirely on systems — no camera, no face, no traditional production costs. The objective is to design, build, and launch a channel from zero using AI-powered content pipelines, automated voiceover tools, and data-driven SEO strategy to build a compounding media asset.

You will select a profitable niche, construct an end-to-end production workflow using tools like ElevenLabs, fal.ai, and CapCut, and establish a batch publishing cadence that lets you produce four videos in the time most creators produce one. Thumbnail generation, caption automation via Whisper AI, and Python-based batch processing are wired into the pipeline to remove manual bottlenecks.

Upon completion you will have a live channel, a full content calendar, and a repeatable system capable of reaching YouTube Partner Program eligibility — 1,000 subscribers and 4,000 watch hours — through algorithmic consistency rather than personal charisma. This is infrastructure thinking applied to media creation.

Agent Requirements
  • Comfortable using AI tools — ChatGPT, Claude, or similar LLMs for writing
  • Basic familiarity with video editing concepts (cuts, audio sync, export settings)
  • A Google account to create and manage the YouTube channel
  • Budget awareness: free tier tools are available throughout — paid tools are optional upgrades
  • Basic Python knowledge is helpful for Phase 5 automation but not required for core phases
  • Willingness to publish publicly — this project produces a real, live channel
Operation Phases
Progress
0 / 6
✓
1
Niche Research & Channel Strategy
Research profitable faceless niches: tech explainers, finance tips, history docs, motivation, scary stories, AI news, coding tutorials. Analyze 5 competitor channels for subscriber count, view averages, upload frequency, and content style. Define 3-4 recurring content pillars and lock in your channel name, tagline, brand colors, font style, and tone of voice. Build a mood board of visual references that will anchor your production aesthetic.
Checkpoint
You should have a 1-page channel brief: niche, target audience, 3 content pillars, channel name, and a mood board of visual references. If you cannot summarize your niche in one sentence and name your top 3 competitor channels, go back — clarity at this stage prevents wasted production effort in every phase that follows.
✓
2
AI Content Pipeline Setup
Set up text-to-speech: ElevenLabs (paid, best quality), Edge TTS (free, decent), or Coqui TTS (open source). Set up image/video generation: fal.ai, Midjourney, Pexels/Pixabay for stock footage, or Canva for motion graphics. Configure script writing via Claude API, ChatGPT, or a local LLM — create a reusable prompt template tuned to your niche. Create your folder structure: /scripts, /voiceovers, /visuals, /exports, /thumbnails. Test the full pipeline end-to-end with a 60-second test clip.
Checkpoint
You should be able to go from a topic idea to a rough 60-second video clip using your AI tools in under 30 minutes. Run the test: pick a topic, generate a script with your LLM, synthesize the voiceover, source 4-5 matching visuals, and stitch them in your editor. If any step takes more than 10 minutes, it needs optimization before you scale to full-length videos.
✓
3
Video Production Workflow
Script writing formula: hook (first 8 seconds), problem, story/content, CTA. Record voiceover from script using your TTS tool, export as WAV/MP3. Source or generate visuals with 1 change every 3-5 seconds to maintain engagement. Edit in CapCut (free), DaVinci Resolve (pro), or ffmpeg (automation). Add royalty-free background music from Epidemic Sound, Artlist, or YouTube Audio Library. Add captions using CapCut auto-captions or Whisper AI. Create a thumbnail: bold text (3-5 words max), high contrast, 1280x720.
Checkpoint
Produce one complete 5-8 minute video with voiceover, visuals synced every 3-5 seconds, background music mixed under the voice, captions on screen, and a finished thumbnail at 1280x720. Watch it back in full before moving on — identify at least one thing you would do differently in the next video. That self-critique note becomes your improvement log.
✓
4
Channel Setup & SEO
Create the YouTube channel with an optimized About section (keywords in the first 2 lines). Design a channel banner at 2560x1440 and profile picture at 800x800 matching your brand identity from Phase 1. Run keyword research using TubeBuddy, VidIQ, or YouTube search autocomplete. Title formula: [Number/Power Word] + [Topic] + [Benefit/Curiosity Gap]. Description template: first 2 lines are hook and keyword, then timestamps, links, and hashtags. Use 8-12 tags mixing broad and specific terms. Configure default upload settings — description template, tags, end screen, cards.
Checkpoint
Channel is live with banner, profile picture, About section, and default upload settings configured. You can explain your title and thumbnail strategy for any topic in your niche without hesitation. Pick a topic right now and write a title following the formula — if it takes more than 2 minutes, practice the formula on 5 more topics before moving to Phase 5.
✓
5
Automation & Batch Production
Build a batch workflow: write 4 scripts in one session, record 4 voiceovers, edit 4 videos. Create a Notion or spreadsheet content calendar tracking topic, script status, edit status, and publish date. Schedule uploads using YouTube Studio — optimal windows are typically Tuesday, Thursday, and Saturday mornings. Set up the YouTube Analytics dashboard to monitor watch time, CTR, retention graph, and subscriber sources. Automate thumbnail generation using Canva batch or a Python + Pillow template script that fills a base design with your video title automatically.
Checkpoint
You have 4 videos queued and scheduled for the next 2 weeks. Your content calendar has topics planned for the next 30 days. The thumbnail automation script (or Canva template) can generate a new thumbnail in under 2 minutes given a title. If scheduling 4 videos took more than one full day of work, identify the bottleneck step — that is what to automate next.
✓
6
Launch, Analyze, Iterate
Publish your first 3 videos within the first week — momentum matters for the algorithm. After each video check: retention graph (where do viewers drop off?), CTR (aim for 4-10%), and average view duration. A/B test thumbnails using YouTube's built-in test feature. Engage with every comment in the first 48 hours for an algorithm boost. After 10 videos, identify your best performer, analyze why it worked, and make more like it. Monetization path: 1,000 subscribers + 4,000 watch hours = YouTube Partner Program. Alternative revenue: affiliate links in description, Patreon, digital products, sponsorships.
Checkpoint
10 videos published. You have a spreadsheet tracking each video's CTR, retention percentage, and view count. You have identified your top performer and written a 1-paragraph analysis of why it outperformed the others — covering title, thumbnail, hook quality, and topic demand. That analysis drives your next 10 videos. No analysis means no compounding improvement.
