Where this data comes from

Wage figures on this page are derived from the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics, Occupational Employment and Wage Statistics (OEWS) program, May 2024 national estimates. Growth projections come from the BLS Employment Projections program for the 2024 to 2034 decade. Role descriptions and on-the-job tasks are drawn from the O*NET 28.3 database, sponsored by the U.S. Department of Labor.

Primary sources:

  • BLS OEWS, May 2024 national estimates by SOC code
  • BLS Occupational Outlook Handbook, Information Security Analysts (SOC 15-1212)
  • BLS Employment Projections, 2024 to 2034
  • O*NET Online (U.S. Department of Labor)

Wages shown are approximate national figures rounded to the nearest thousand and reflect commonly reported BLS OEWS estimates for the listed SOC codes. Individual salaries vary by metropolitan area, employer, clearance level, and specialization. Hexworth Prime does not guarantee outcomes. Always verify against the live BLS source linked above before salary negotiation.
