Operation Ghost Haul
7 high-value electronics loads stolen in 3 weeks. Every time, someone shows up with the correct BOL, MC number, and driver name. The real carriers arrive to empty docks. Someone is inside the TMS.
INCIDENT BRIEFING — VP LOGISTICS, MERIDIAN FREIGHT SOLUTIONS

Over the past 3 weeks, 7 shipments of consumer electronics have been stolen during pickup. In each case, an individual presented the correct Bill of Lading number, Motor Carrier number, and driver name at the shipper's dock. The shipper released the load. When the actual assigned carrier arrived 30-60 minutes later, the load was already gone.

Total losses: $1.4M across 7 loads. All electronics (TVs, laptops, networking equipment). All picked up within 48 hours of being scheduled in our Transportation Management System (FleetCommand TMS).

The attacker has real-time access to our load schedules.

Objectives: Determine how the attacker accessed load data. Identify the compromised account. Map the attack chain from initial access to load theft.
