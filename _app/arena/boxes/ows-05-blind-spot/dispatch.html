Carrier Error Theory (Initial Assessment)
Initial theory from NFP management: legitimate carriers were subcontracting loads without authorization. Pacific Coast Lines and Eagle Transport were both contacted. Both carriers confirmed they never received the dispatch notifications for the 47 affected loads. They were not subcontracting — they were never in the loop. This theory does not hold.
