INCIDENT BRIEFING — VAULTGUARD DAO GOVERNANCE

Six hours ago, a single transaction drained 4,200 ETH ($14.7M at current prices) from the VaultGuard lending protocol. The exploit used a reentrancy attack through the _beforeTokenTransfer hook — a function added in a recent code update that passed a third-party security audit.

The funds moved through 6 intermediate wallets in rapid succession, then entered Tornado Cash as 42 deposits of 100 ETH each. Withdrawals are ongoing. An exchange has flagged a KYC account receiving funds from Tornado Cash exit addresses.

Your mission: Trace the funds from exploit to cash-out. Identify the vulnerability, the attacker, and how they got the malicious code past the audit.

Objectives: Identify the exploit type. Trace the fund flow. Unmask the attacker.
