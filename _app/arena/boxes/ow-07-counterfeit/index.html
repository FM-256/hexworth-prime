NATO COOPERATIVE CYBER DEFENCE CENTRE OF EXCELLENCE

Three weeks before a NATO ally's parliamentary election, a coordinated influence operation has flooded social media with fabricated stories targeting the leading candidate. The operation uses 200+ fake accounts with AI-generated profile photos, 20 fake news websites, and $340K in targeted ad spending across multiple platforms.

The content is professionally produced, A/B tested, and translated into the target country's language — but the translations contain artifacts suggesting a non-native source language.

Objectives: Attribute the campaign. Identify the threat actor. Trace the funding. Map the infrastructure.
