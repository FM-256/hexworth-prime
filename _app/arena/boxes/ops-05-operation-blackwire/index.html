Situation Briefing

Meridian Dynamics — a Tier 1 defense contractor responsible for satellite communication systems, encrypted field radios, and GPS augmentation for special operations forces — reported anomalous network activity 6 hours ago.


Initial triage by their internal SOC revealed indicators consistent with APT group BLACKWIRE, a nation-state actor known for targeting defense industrial base organizations. The group has been inside the network for an estimated 47 days.


Your team has been embedded as the external incident response unit. You have forensic access to systems across the Meridian Dynamics network. A secure C2 channel has been established for flag submission and intelligence drops.
