Operation Paper Trail
12 loads stolen from Summit Logistics in 6 weeks. A phantom carrier with cloned MC numbers shows up with perfect paperwork before the real carrier arrives. Someone is intercepting load tenders.
INCIDENT BRIEFING — SUMMIT LOGISTICS CEO

Summit Logistics is a mid-size freight broker handling 200+ loads daily. In the past 6 weeks, 12 loads totaling $1.8M have been stolen at pickup. In every case, an unauthorized carrier ("Atlas Express LLC") arrived with the correct Bill of Lading, correct MC number, and correct load details — and the shipper released the goods.

Our dispatchers assigned these loads to legitimate carriers. But Atlas Express received the load information before our real carriers were notified. Someone is intercepting our communications.

Objectives: Find the email compromise. Identify the phantom carrier. Trace the money.
