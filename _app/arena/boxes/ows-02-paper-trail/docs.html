Investigation Note: Shipper Complicity Theory
Initial Theory: Shippers (dock staff) were intentionally releasing loads to unauthorized carriers. Possible inside job at shipper facilities.

Evidence Review:
- 5 different shipper facilities across GA and FL were affected
- Dock staff at all locations followed standard release protocol:
  1. Verified carrier name matched BOL
  2. Verified MC number in FMCSA SAFER (showed "AUTHORIZED")
  3. Verified driver had matching CDL
  4. Loaded trailer and applied seal
- The phantom carrier presented CORRECT documentation every time
- No shipper employee had advance knowledge of carrier assignments

Conclusion: Shipper complicity ruled out. Dock staff were deceived by legitimate-looking documents generated from intercepted email data. The breach is upstream at the broker level.
[Pin: Shipper complicity theory — ruled out, shippers were deceived]
