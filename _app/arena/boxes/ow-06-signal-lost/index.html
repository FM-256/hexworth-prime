Operation Signal Lost
MQ-9B surveillance drone RAVEN-7 went dark over contested airspace. Initial assessment: SAM engagement. But the telemetry tells a different story.
TACTICAL OPERATIONS CENTER — COL. HARTWELL (J2 INTELLIGENCE)

RAVEN-7, an MQ-9B Block 5 surveillance drone, lost contact during a routine ISR mission over contested airspace at 0417Z today. Initial assessment assumed SA-22 Greyhound SAM engagement based on the asset operating in a known threat ring.

However, the Missile Approach Warning System (MAWS) did not activate. No IR plume detected. No radar lock warnings. The telemetry shows something unusual — a controlled descent, not a crash. The satellite communication module rebooted mid-flight. And there's no debris at the expected impact point.

Objectives: Determine cause of loss. Locate the asset. Identify the adversary unit responsible.
