What this console is for

Every reading here arrived through something: a link, a clock, a signing authority. The console tells you which, on every line, because that is the difference between two sources and one source counted twice.
Type lehelp to begin. Network tooling (nmap, tcpdump, dig) works here too: the ground segment is a real network.
