Incident Brief

You are duty incident response lead. You hold delegated commercial operator authority, not state authority, and not the only certificate ASTRAEA-9 will accept.
Everything you see arrived through something. A sensor, a log, a gateway, a certificate. Nothing on this console is direct observation, and at this distance nothing is current. Decide what still deserves trust.
