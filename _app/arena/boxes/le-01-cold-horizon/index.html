Incident Brief

ASTRAEA-9 is an uncrewed orbital edge-compute platform holding station near Earth–Moon L1. At 04:12Z the platform reported a thermal excursion, and its automation began preparing a containment action. Ground telemetry and platform telemetry do not agree.
You are duty incident response lead. You hold delegated commercial operator authority, not state authority, and not the only certificate ASTRAEA-9 will accept.
Everything you see arrived through something. A sensor, a log, a gateway, a certificate. Nothing on this console is direct observation, and at this distance nothing is current. Decide what still deserves trust.

Fly

Take RSV-04 out and observe the thing yourself. A flown observation shares no link, no clock and no signing authority with the platform, which is the only reason it can corroborate anything the platform tells you.

Work the ground segment

Reason at the desk: compare sources, test whether two records could have failed independently, and file a finding. Every reading here carries the link, the clock and the authority it arrived by.
