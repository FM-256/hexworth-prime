CASE BRIEFING — FINCEN FINANCIAL CRIMES UNIT

Pacific Coast Regional Bank has been flagged for anomalous transaction patterns. Over $47M has flowed through 12 accounts in 18 months — all structured just below Currency Transaction Report thresholds. The compliance team filed 14 Suspicious Activity Reports, but all were internally dismissed by the same VP.

The 12 accounts belong to shell companies that trace through layered ownership to an offshore entity in Cyprus. The beneficial owner is on the OFAC sanctions list.

Objectives: Map the laundering method. Identify the bank insider. Trace the money flow to its endpoint.
