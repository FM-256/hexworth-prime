02 · The Alliance Program

Five ways to work together

Each alliance answers the same four questions — what you're up against, how Hexworth helps, what a partnership looks like, and how to begin.

Academic Alliance

Universities & educators

Colleges, universities, and instructors
The challenge
Keeping cybersecurity and technology curriculum current, hands-on, and certification-aligned — without building lab infrastructure from scratch.
How Hexworth helps
A 12-house curriculum with browser-based labs, CTF challenges, and certification-aligned paths; instructor dashboards, white-label institutional tenants, and learner progress analytics.
What a partnership looks like
Classroom pilots, curriculum integration, co-branded or white-label deployments, and cohort programs.
How to begin
Request a pilot or a walkthrough.
Technology Alliance

Cloud, security & product providers

Cloud, security, hardware, and software companies
The challenge
Reaching the next generation of practitioners and getting real tools into hands-on training environments.
How Hexworth helps
Teaching on real tools and workflows, with labs and challenges that can feature partner technologies, in front of an audience actively building cloud, security, and AI skills.
What a partnership looks like
Cloud credits and infrastructure collaborations, product evaluations, curriculum integration, and developer advocacy.
How to begin
Explore a technical collaboration.
Workforce Alliance

Workforce & reskilling programs

Workforce development, veteran, and reskilling organizations
The challenge
Reskilling talent into the technology workforce with measurable outcomes — not just seat time.
How Hexworth helps
Structured pathways from foundations to certification-aligned skills, connected to the Career Launchpad (résumé and cover-letter tools, curated job-search launchers, and interview prep) so learning links toward employment.
What a partnership looks like
Cohort and reskilling programs, veteran and workforce initiatives, and outcome-focused pilots.
How to begin
Design a cohort or pilot together.
Research Alliance

Faculty & AI-in-education research

Universities, faculty, and education researchers
The challenge
Studying AI-assisted learning and skill acquisition needs a real platform and ethically-handled data.
How Hexworth helps
An AI-native education platform and a genuine interest in collaborating on applied research in AI-assisted education and cybersecurity learning.
What a partnership looks like
Research collaborations, co-authored studies, grant collaborations, and ethical data partnerships.
How to begin
Propose a research collaboration.
Strategic Alliance

Certification bodies & employers

Certification organizations, major providers, and employers
The challenge
Building a reliable pipeline of verified, job-ready technical talent.
How Hexworth helps
Certification-aligned curriculum, verified evidence through assessments and badges, and a career layer that guides learners toward employment.
What a partnership looks like
Certification and curriculum partnerships, employer talent pipelines, co-marketing, and deeper strategic initiatives as traction grows.
How to begin
Open a strategic conversation.
