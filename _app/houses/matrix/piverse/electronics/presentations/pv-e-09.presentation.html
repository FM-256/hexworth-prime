Power Management
3.3V vs 5V, USB-C PD, battery operation, current budgeting, safe shutdown.
Power management is the least glamorous and most critical skill. Understanding voltage rails, current budgets, battery options, and safe shutdown prevents brownouts, SD card corruption, and hardware damage.

Pi Power Requirements
Each model draws different current. Your supply must exceed total system demand.
Pi 4
5.1V/3A recommended (USB-C). Idle: 600mA. Load: 1.2A.
Pi 5
5V/5A recommended (USB-C PD). Idle: 800mA. Load: 2A.
Pi Zero 2 W
5V/1.2A (Micro USB). Idle: 150mA. Great for battery projects.

3.3V vs 5V Rails
Different rails, different purposes, different limits.
3.3V Rail
Regulated. ~800mA max total. Powers GPIO logic, I2C, 3.3V sensors. Do NOT power motors from this.
5V Rail
Direct from USB-C input. Powers servos, relays, LED strips. Current limited by your PSU minus Pi draw.

Current Budgeting
Add every component, add 25% margin, size your supply.
Power Budget: Pi 4 active 1200 mA 4x LEDs 20 mA Sense HAT 50 mA Servo (moving) 500 mA -------- Total: 1770 mA With 25% margin: 2213 mA PSU needed: 5V/3A

Battery Options
USB power banks, LiPo + boost, 18650 cells.
Power Banks
Simplest. 10,000mAh = ~4h on Pi 4. Need always-on mode.
LiPo + Boost
3.7V battery + boost to 5V. Adafruit PowerBoost. Compact.
18650 Cells
High capacity (3500mAh each). TP4056 charger + boost. Parallel for runtime.

Safe Shutdown
Never yank power. Use hardware shutdown buttons and scripts.
# GPIO3 shutdown overlay: dtoverlay=gpio-shutdown,gpio_pin=3 # Python shutdown button: from gpiozero import Button from subprocess import call btn = Button(21, hold_time=3) btn.when_held = lambda: call(["sudo","shutdown","-h","now"])

UPS Solutions

DIY UPS

Powering External Devices
Motors and LED strips need separate power. Share only GND.
1Separate supply for motors. Dedicated 5V/12V. Only GND + control signals to Pi.
2Neopixels: 60mA per pixel at white. 60 pixels = 3.6A. Separate 5V/4A supply.
3Common ground: All devices MUST share GND. Without it, signals are undefined.
Slide 9 of 12
Power Filtering
Clean power prevents sensor noise and communication errors.
Decoupling Caps
100nF ceramic near every IC. 10uF electrolytic on power rails. Absorbs switching noise.
Ferrite Beads
In series with power lines. Block high-frequency noise while passing DC. Isolate motor power from sensors.
Slide 10 of 12
Brownout Detection
Recognize and prevent undervoltage conditions.
1Lightning bolt icon: Appears on screen when input drops below 4.63V. Immediate action required.
2SD card corruption: Brownouts during writes corrupt the filesystem. Use safe shutdown.
3Check with: vcgencmd get_throttled. Bit 0 = undervoltage detected.
Slide 11 of 12
Runtime Calculation
Battery capacity, converter efficiency, and estimated hours.
Runtime Formula: Hours = (Capacity_mAh * Efficiency) / Current_mA Example: 10,000 mAh * 0.80 efficiency / 1200 mA = 6.7 hours Pi Zero 2 W on same bank: 10,000 * 0.85 / 200 = 42.5 hours

Key Takeaways: Power Management
Five essential facts from this chapter.
1Budget your current. Add all draws + 25% margin. Brownouts corrupt SD cards.
23.3V rail is limited. ~800mA max. Logic and sensors only. Never motors.
3Separate power for high-current loads. Share only GND with the Pi.
4Always safe shutdown. dtoverlay=gpio-shutdown or Python script. Never yank power.
5Runtime = capacity * efficiency / current. Factor in converter losses (80-90%).
