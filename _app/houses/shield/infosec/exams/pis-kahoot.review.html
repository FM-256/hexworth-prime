20 questions spanning all four weeks. Each correct answer earns up to 1,000 points. Faster correct answers score more. Build streaks for bonus points. Projector-ready for live classroom use.

How to Play

  • 30 seconds per question; the timer bar drains left to right
  • Correct answer earns 500 to 1,000 points depending on speed
  • From the 3rd correct answer onward, each correct earns a +200 streak bonus
  • Wrong answer, timeout, or skip earns 0 points and resets the streak
  • Host can pause the timer or skip a question at any time
  • 20 questions drawn from all four PIS weeks
