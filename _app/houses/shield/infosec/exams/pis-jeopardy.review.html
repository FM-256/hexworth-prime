Principles of Information Security: Jeopardy Review

Review the whole PIS course in classic Jeopardy style. Five categories spanning CIA fundamentals, threats, networks, OS hardening, and the security mindset. Pick a category, read the clue, formulate your response in the form of a question. Clear the board.

~30 min Game All Levels

How to Play

  • Select any cell on the board to reveal a Jeopardy-style clue
  • The clue is phrased as an "answer", and you must think of the matching "question"
  • Click Reveal Response to see the correct question (or press Space / Enter)
  • Self-judge: mark correct to earn the value, or wrong to lose it
  • Higher values = harder clues, so start low if warming up
  • Build a streak of correct answers for bonus glory
  • One cell on the board is the Daily Double: revealed only when clicked. Wager any amount from $5 up to the higher of your current score or $500. Correct = win your wager, wrong = lose it.
  • Clear all 25 cells to see your final score and category breakdown
Jeopardy Format Reminder

In Jeopardy, the board shows "answers" and contestants respond with "questions." For example, if the clue says "This CIA component ensures only authorized users can read the data", the correct response would be "What is Confidentiality?" (always phrased as a question).
