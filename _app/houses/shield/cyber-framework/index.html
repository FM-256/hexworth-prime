About This Course

The Law and Policy of Cybersecurity explores the legal, regulatory, and policy landscape surrounding information security. From the Computer Fraud and Abuse Act to NIST frameworks, from encryption debates to international cyber warfare law, these 8 micromodules provide essential knowledge for cybersecurity professionals who need to understand the legal context of their work.

Each micromodule includes a presentation, interactive lab, and quiz. A comprehensive Jeopardy review ties it all together. Complete all 25 activities to master the curriculum.
