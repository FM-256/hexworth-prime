Risk Register Builder

You are the risk analyst for Meridian Financial Services. Eleven risks have been identified in this quarter's assessment. Assign Likelihood and Impact ratings, select the correct risk treatment, and build the organization's risk register.

How this lab works: For each identified risk below:
1. Set Likelihood (1-5) and Impact (1-5) using the sliders -- the lab computes the risk score (L x I) and plots it live on the heat-map to the right.
2. Choose the correct Risk Treatment from the four SY0-701 strategies.
3. If you choose Mitigate, also select the expected Residual Risk Band after controls are applied.
Your L/I rating is graded against a defensible range; the treatment choice is graded as correct or incorrect. A score of 80% or higher unlocks Mark Complete.

Risk Score Formula

Every risk is rated on two axes:

Risk Score = Likelihood x Impact

Low 1-4  |  Medium 5-9  |  High 10-14  |  Critical 15-25

Inherent vs Residual

Inherent risk -- the raw risk score before any controls are applied. This is what you rate with the sliders.

Residual risk -- the risk that remains AFTER controls are implemented. Never zero.

Risk Appetite vs Tolerance

Appetite -- how much risk the org is willing to pursue to achieve its goals (strategic level).

Tolerance -- the acceptable deviation around the appetite (operational band). Accept is only valid when residual risk falls within tolerance.

The 4 Risk Treatments

Mitigate Reduce likelihood or impact via controls (patches, MFA, encryption).

Transfer Shift financial impact to a third party (cyber-insurance, outsourcing).

Avoid Stop or forgo the risky activity entirely.

Accept Formally tolerate when residual risk is within appetite/tolerance (cost of control exceeds benefit).
