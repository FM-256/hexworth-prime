Policy Hierarchy

Policy
High-level management directive stating intent and objectives. Sets the WHAT and WHY — not the how.
Standard
Specific, mandatory, measurable requirement derived from a policy (e.g., "AES-256 required").
Procedure
Ordered, step-by-step instructions for carrying out a task — the HOW. Must be followed exactly.
Guideline
Non-mandatory best-practice advice. Advisory, not enforceable — "consider" or "recommended" language.

Data Roles

Data Owner
Senior business executive ultimately accountable for a data set. Sets classification level and approves access.
Data Custodian
IT role responsible for day-to-day technical protection (backups, encryption, ACLs) per the owner's direction.
Data Processor
External entity that handles personal data strictly on behalf of, and under instruction from, the data controller.

Asset Lifecycle

Asset Sanitization / Disposal
End-of-life process ensuring data is irreversibly destroyed before equipment leaves the organization (crypto-erase, degauss, shred, certificate of destruction).

Exam Strategy

Policy vs. Standard vs. Procedure
Policy = intent (WHAT/WHY). Standard = mandatory number/spec. Procedure = exact steps. Guideline = optional advice.
Owner vs. Custodian vs. Processor
Owner = business accountability. Custodian = IT implementation. Processor = third-party handler under contract.
