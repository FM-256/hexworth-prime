Why Email Security Matters

Email remains the number one attack vector in enterprise environments. Over 90% of successful breaches begin with a phishing email. Microsoft Defender for Office 365 (MDO) protects Exchange Online, SharePoint Online, OneDrive for Business, and Microsoft Teams from malicious content -- phishing URLs, weaponized attachments, business email compromise (BEC), and impersonation attacks.

MDO operates in two tiers. Plan 1 provides protection features (Safe Links, Safe Attachments, anti-phishing). Plan 2 adds investigation and response capabilities (Threat Explorer, Automated Investigation and Response, Attack Simulation Training). Most E5 licenses include Plan 2.

Safe Links

Safe Links provides time-of-click URL verification. When a user clicks a link in an email, Teams message, or Office document, the URL is checked against Microsoft's threat intelligence in real time. If the URL is malicious, the user sees a warning page instead of being redirected to the phishing site.

Setting What It Does Recommended
URL rewriting Rewrites URLs to route through Microsoft's Safe Links service On (default in preset policies)
Real-time URL scanning Scans URLs at click time, even if they were clean at delivery On -- catches delayed weaponization
Wait for URL scanning Blocks navigation until scan completes On -- prevents race conditions
Do not track user clicks Disables click tracking for privacy Off -- click data is valuable for investigations
Do not rewrite internal URLs Skips Safe Links for intra-organization URLs Depends on environment; internal phishing exists
Delayed Weaponization

Attackers frequently send emails with benign URLs, wait for delivery to pass scanning, then change the destination to a phishing page. Safe Links' time-of-click scanning defeats this technique because the URL is re-evaluated every time a user clicks it, not just at delivery time.

Safe Attachments

Safe Attachments detonates email attachments in a sandbox environment before delivering them to the user's mailbox. The attachment is opened in a virtual machine where its behavior is observed -- does it launch PowerShell? Does it reach out to a C2 server? Does it modify the registry? If malicious behavior is detected, the attachment is blocked.

Monitor

Delivers the message and tracks results. No blocking. Used for initial testing to understand what would be caught without impacting mail flow.

Block

Blocks messages with detected malicious attachments. The message is quarantined and the recipient never sees it. Recommended for production.

Replace

Delivers the email body but removes the malicious attachment. A notification replaces the attachment explaining it was blocked.

Dynamic Delivery

Delivers the email immediately with a placeholder attachment. The real attachment is attached after scanning completes. Best balance of security and user experience.

Safe Attachments also extends to SharePoint, OneDrive, and Teams. Files uploaded to these services are scanned asynchronously. Malicious files are blocked from being downloaded or shared, but the file remains visible (locked) so admins can investigate.

Anti-Phishing Policies

MDO's anti-phishing capabilities go beyond simple URL and attachment scanning. They detect impersonation attacks, spoofing, and social engineering techniques:

Protection What It Detects
User impersonation Emails where the sender name closely resembles a protected user (e.g., "John Smith" vs "J0hn Smith"). Protects C-suite, finance, and HR.
Domain impersonation Emails from domains that look like your organization's domain (e.g., contoso.com vs c0ntoso.com or contoso-inc.com)
Mailbox intelligence ML model that learns each user's communication patterns. Flags messages from unfamiliar senders that impersonate known contacts.
Spoof intelligence Detects emails where the From header domain does not match the sending infrastructure (SPF/DKIM/DMARC failures)
First contact safety tip Shows a banner when a user receives email from someone they have never communicated with before
Business Email Compromise (BEC)

BEC attacks do not use malware or malicious links. Instead, an attacker impersonates a CEO or CFO and sends a plain-text email requesting a wire transfer or sensitive data. These attacks bypass traditional email filters. MDO's impersonation protection and mailbox intelligence are specifically designed to catch BEC by analyzing sender patterns and display name similarity.

Threat Explorer

Threat Explorer (Plan 2) is the investigation tool for email-based threats. It provides a searchable view of all emails processed by MDO with filtering by sender, recipient, subject, detection technology, delivery action, and time range.

Key investigation workflows in Threat Explorer:

Phishing Campaign Analysis

Filter by detection = Phishing, group by sender domain or subject line. Identify how many users received the phishing email, how many clicked, and which ones submitted credentials.

Email Entity Investigation

Click any email to see its full headers, authentication results (SPF/DKIM/DMARC), detonation results, URL analysis, and delivery timeline. The email entity page is the single-email investigation hub.

Remediation Actions

Select emails and soft-delete them from all recipient mailboxes, move to junk, or hard-delete. Critical for cleaning up phishing campaigns that reached inboxes.

URL Click Analysis

View all Safe Links click events. Identify which users clicked malicious URLs, when they clicked, and whether the click was blocked or allowed.

Email Authentication

Understanding email authentication protocols is essential for investigating spoofing and impersonation:

Protocol What It Validates Limitation
SPF Sending server IP is authorized for the envelope sender domain Does not validate the From header domain (what users see)
DKIM Email body and headers have not been tampered with; signed by the sending domain Does not prevent display name impersonation
DMARC Aligns SPF or DKIM with the From header domain; specifies policy (none/quarantine/reject) Requires both SPF/DKIM deployed; adoption still incomplete

Key Takeaways

  • Safe Links provides time-of-click URL verification, defeating delayed weaponization attacks
  • Safe Attachments detonates files in a sandbox; Dynamic Delivery balances security with user experience
  • Anti-phishing policies detect impersonation, spoofing, and BEC using ML and pattern analysis
  • Threat Explorer is the investigation hub for email threats -- use it to trace campaigns and remediate
  • SPF, DKIM, and DMARC are complementary email authentication layers; DMARC alignment is the goal
