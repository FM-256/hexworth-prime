Microsoft Purview and Data Loss Prevention

Learning Objectives

  • Understand the Microsoft Purview platform and its compliance capabilities

  • Design Data Loss Prevention (DLP) policies for email, SharePoint, and endpoints

What is Microsoft Purview?

Microsoft Purview is the unified data governance, risk, and compliance platform. It was created by merging Azure Purview (data catalog) with Microsoft 365 compliance features. For security analysts, Purview handles the data protection side of security -- ensuring sensitive information is classified, labeled, and prevented from leaving the organization through unauthorized channels.

While Defender products focus on detecting and stopping attacks, Purview focuses on protecting the data itself. Even if an attacker gains access, properly labeled and encrypted documents remain protected. And if an insider attempts to exfiltrate data, DLP policies detect and block the action.

Sensitivity Labels

Sensitivity labels classify and optionally protect documents and emails based on their content sensitivity. Labels are defined by administrators and applied by users (manually or automatically). A label can apply:

Visual Markings

Headers, footers, and watermarks added to documents. Example: "CONFIDENTIAL" watermark on every page. Helps users understand the document's classification at a glance.

Encryption

Azure Rights Management (RMS) encryption applied to the document. Controls who can open, edit, print, or forward. Encryption persists even if the file is copied outside the organization.

Content Marking

Metadata embedded in the document that other systems (DLP, CASB, email rules) can read and act on. The label travels with the document regardless of where it moves.

Access Controls

Granular permissions: "Marketing team can edit; everyone else view-only; external users cannot open." Enforced by the Azure RMS service, not by the application.

A typical label hierarchy in an enterprise:

Label Protection Usage
Public None Press releases, marketing materials, public documentation
General / Internal Visual marking only Internal memos, team wikis, non-sensitive business data
Confidential Encryption + marking Financial reports, HR records, strategy documents
Highly Confidential Encryption + restricted access + marking M&A plans, legal holds, executive communications, PII databases
Auto-Labeling

Purview can automatically apply sensitivity labels based on content inspection. Trainable classifiers and sensitive information types (SITs) scan documents for patterns like Social Security numbers, credit card numbers, or custom data patterns. Auto-labeling can work in simulation mode first, showing what would be labeled without actually applying labels.

Data Loss Prevention (DLP)

DLP policies detect and prevent the unauthorized sharing of sensitive information. A DLP policy defines: what sensitive data to look for, where to look for it, and what to do when it is found.

Component Configuration
Locations Exchange email, SharePoint sites, OneDrive accounts, Teams chat/channel messages, Windows/macOS endpoints, Power BI
Sensitive Info Types Built-in patterns (SSN, credit card, passport), custom regex patterns, keyword dictionaries, trainable classifiers, exact data match (EDM)
Conditions Content contains X instances of sensitive type, content is shared externally, content has specific sensitivity label
Actions Block sharing, encrypt, notify user, notify admin, generate incident report, restrict to specific users
User Notifications Policy tips in Outlook/SharePoint/Teams warning users before they share sensitive content. Users can override with justification.

DLP policies operate at different locations with different enforcement capabilities:

Exchange Online DLP

Scans email body and attachments for sensitive content. Can block the email, redirect to compliance officer, or add encryption. Transport rules can augment DLP with header modifications.

Endpoint DLP

Monitors file operations on Windows/macOS devices: copy to USB, upload to cloud storage, print, copy to clipboard. Extends DLP beyond cloud services to the device itself.

SharePoint/OneDrive DLP

Scans documents stored in SharePoint libraries and OneDrive. Blocks external sharing of files containing sensitive data. Works with sensitivity labels for compound policies.

Teams DLP

Scans chat messages and channel messages for sensitive content. Can block the message and show a policy tip to the sender. Does not scan files shared in Teams (handled by SharePoint DLP).

Insider Risk Management

Purview's Insider Risk Management uses behavioral signals to detect potential insider threats: data theft by departing employees, accidental data leaks, security policy violations, and IP theft. It correlates HR signals (resignation, termination) with data activity signals (mass file downloads, USB copies, printing spikes).

Key concepts: risk indicators (specific events that contribute to risk score), policies (define which indicators and users to monitor), and alerts (generated when a user's cumulative risk exceeds the threshold). Investigations include a timeline of all the user's risky activities with supporting evidence.

Audit and eDiscovery

The Unified Audit Log records user and admin activity across Microsoft 365 services. Every file access, email send, permission change, and admin action is logged. Standard retention is 180 days; E5 licenses extend to 1 year with the option for 10-year retention add-on.

eDiscovery (Electronic Discovery) is used for legal investigations and regulatory requests. Content Search finds emails, documents, and Teams messages matching search criteria. eDiscovery cases preserve content under legal hold, preventing deletion by users or retention policies. Premium eDiscovery adds review sets, analytics, and predictive coding for large-scale document review.

Key Takeaways

  • Sensitivity labels classify data and optionally apply encryption, visual markings, and access controls
  • DLP policies prevent unauthorized sharing across email, SharePoint, OneDrive, Teams, and endpoints
  • Sensitive information types (built-in and custom) are the detection engine for both labels and DLP
  • Insider Risk Management correlates HR signals with data activity to detect internal threats
  • Audit logs and eDiscovery provide the investigation backbone for compliance and legal requirements
