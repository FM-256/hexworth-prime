Microsoft Purview Compliance Portal
The central hub for compliance management across Microsoft 365. Access compliance solutions, manage policies, and monitor compliance posture from a single portal.
Compliance Manager
Dashboard that measures your compliance posture with a compliance score. Similar to Defender for Cloud's secure score but focused on regulatory compliance (GDPR, HIPAA, ISO 27001). Provides improvement actions with step-by-step guidance.
Compliance Score
Numerical score (0-100%) based on completed improvement actions. Microsoft-managed actions (controls Microsoft implements) contribute to your baseline score. Customer-managed actions (controls you implement) increase your score further.
Assessments
Pre-built templates for 300+ regulations and standards. Each assessment maps Microsoft 365 controls to regulatory requirements. Track progress per regulation. Custom assessments for industry-specific requirements.

Data Classification
Before you can protect data, you must classify it. Microsoft Purview provides built-in and custom classifiers to automatically identify sensitive information across Microsoft 365.
Sensitive Information Types
Pattern-based classifiers that detect specific data: credit card numbers, Social Security numbers, passport numbers, bank account numbers. 300+ built-in types. Custom types using regex, keywords, and confidence levels.
Trainable Classifiers
Machine learning classifiers trained on sample data. Pre-trained classifiers detect: resumes, source code, harassment, threats, profanity. Custom trainable classifiers learn from your organization's specific data patterns.
Content Explorer
Browse and search for items that have been classified. See which sensitive information types were detected, which labels are applied, and where sensitive data resides across Exchange, SharePoint, OneDrive, and Teams.
Slide 4 of 12
Sensitivity Labels
Persistent, machine-readable tags that travel with the data. A sensitivity label can encrypt, watermark, restrict access, and control sharing -- regardless of where the document goes.
How Labels Work
Labels are metadata applied to documents and emails. Labels can trigger protection actions: encrypt the file, add a visual marking (header, footer, watermark), restrict access to specific users or groups. Labels persist even when files are shared externally.
Label Policies
Publish labels to users so they appear in Office apps. Policies control: which labels are available to which users, whether a default label is applied, whether users can downgrade or remove labels, and whether justification is required for downgrades.
Auto-Labeling
Automatically apply labels based on content inspection. Service-side auto-labeling scans data at rest in SharePoint/OneDrive/Exchange. Client-side auto-labeling recommends labels as users work in Office applications.
Container Labels
Apply sensitivity labels to Teams, Microsoft 365 Groups, and SharePoint sites. Controls: guest access settings, privacy (public/private), unmanaged device access, and external sharing. Governs the container, not individual files.
Slide 5 of 12
Data Loss Prevention (DLP)
Prevent accidental or intentional sharing of sensitive information. DLP policies detect sensitive data and enforce protective actions across Microsoft 365 services.
DLP Policies
Rules that detect sensitive information types in content and enforce actions. Locations: Exchange email, SharePoint sites, OneDrive accounts, Teams chat/channel messages, and Windows endpoints. Actions: block sharing, encrypt, notify user, alert admin.
Policy Tips
Real-time notifications to users when they attempt to share sensitive data. Educates users in the moment without blocking them. Users can override with business justification. Reduces violations through awareness, not just enforcement.
Endpoint DLP
Extends DLP to Windows and macOS devices. Monitors activities like: copying to USB, printing, uploading to cloud services, copying to clipboard. Enforces policies even when files leave Microsoft 365.

Records Management and Retention
Manage the lifecycle of content: keep what you need for compliance, delete what you do not. Retention policies and retention labels control how long content is kept and what happens when it expires.
Retention Policies
Broad policies applied to entire locations (all Exchange mailboxes, all SharePoint sites). Retain content for a specified period (e.g., 7 years), then optionally delete. Or delete content older than a specified age. Policies override user deletion during the retention period.
Retention Labels
Item-level labels for granular control. Applied to individual documents, emails, or folders. Can mark items as records (immutable -- cannot be edited or deleted until retention expires). Support disposition review before final deletion.
Records Management
Formal records declaration for regulatory compliance. Regulatory records cannot be relabeled or deleted by anyone, including admins. File plan provides a structured view for managing record categories, retention periods, and disposition actions.
Adaptive Scopes
Dynamic scoping for retention policies. Instead of targeting all mailboxes, target mailboxes where Department = Finance. Scope automatically updates as users change departments. Replaces static group-based targeting.

eDiscovery
The process of identifying, collecting, and producing electronically stored information (ESI) for legal proceedings, investigations, or audits.
Content Search
Basic search across Exchange, SharePoint, OneDrive, and Teams. KQL-based queries to find specific content. Export results for review. Available in all Microsoft 365 plans. The starting point for any investigation.
eDiscovery (Standard)
Adds case management. Create cases, add custodians (people of interest), place legal holds to preserve content. Legal holds prevent deletion of relevant content even if retention policies would allow it. Export for legal review.
eDiscovery (Premium)
Advanced capabilities: custodian management, legal hold notifications, review sets with AI-powered relevance scoring, conversation threading for Teams/email threads, near-duplicate detection, and themes analysis. Requires E5 or eDiscovery add-on license.
Slide 8 of 12
Insider Risk Management
Detect and respond to risky activities by people inside your organization. Uses behavioral signals, not content inspection, to identify potential insider threats.
Risk Indicators
Correlates signals from multiple sources: file downloads, USB copies, email to personal accounts, print activity, access pattern changes, resignation signals from HR. Machine learning identifies anomalous combinations, not individual events.
Policy Templates
Pre-built templates: data theft by departing users, data leaks, security policy violations, patient data misuse (healthcare). Triggers can be HR events (resignation, termination notice) or DLP policy matches.
Investigation Workflow
Alerts generate cases for investigation. Activity explorer shows a timeline of risky actions. Pseudonymized by default -- investigator sees "User A" not "Jane Smith" until escalation is justified. Integrates with eDiscovery for deeper investigation.
Communication Compliance
Monitor communications (email, Teams, third-party apps) for policy violations: harassment, threats, regulatory compliance (financial services). Uses classifiers to detect inappropriate content. Reviewers assess flagged messages.
Slide 9 of 12
Azure Policy
Enforce organizational standards and assess compliance for Azure resources. Policies evaluate resources and flag or prevent non-compliant configurations.
Policy Definitions
JSON rules that define what is evaluated and what happens when non-compliant. Effects: Deny (block creation), Audit (log only), DeployIfNotExists (auto-remediate), Modify (change properties). Example: deny VM creation without a required tag.
Policy Initiatives
Groups of related policy definitions assigned together. Built-in initiatives: CIS Benchmark, NIST 800-53, ISO 27001. Each initiative maps to a compliance standard with dozens of individual policy checks.
Compliance Dashboard
Shows compliance state for all assigned policies and initiatives. Drill down to non-compliant resources. Generate compliance reports for auditors. Track compliance over time to demonstrate improvement.
Slide 10 of 12
Resource Locks and Blueprints
Prevent accidental deletion or modification of critical Azure resources. Blueprints package policies, role assignments, and ARM templates for repeatable environment deployments.
Resource Locks
Two lock types: CanNotDelete (read and modify allowed, delete blocked) and ReadOnly (read only, no modifications or deletions). Applied at subscription, resource group, or individual resource level. Locks inherit downward.
Azure Blueprints
Define a repeatable set of Azure resources with governance. A blueprint packages: ARM templates, policy assignments, role assignments, and resource groups. Version-controlled and auditable. Deploy consistent environments across subscriptions.
Management Groups
Organize subscriptions into a hierarchy. Apply Azure Policy and RBAC at the management group level -- all child subscriptions inherit. Root management group at the top. Up to 6 levels of depth (excluding root and subscription).

Compliance Products Map
Which product handles which compliance need? This is the key mental model for the compliance portion of the SC-900 exam.
Compliance Posture
Compliance Manager: compliance score, assessments, improvement actions. Your compliance dashboard.
Data Protection
Sensitivity labels + DLP policies. Classify, label, encrypt, and prevent data loss across M365.
Data Lifecycle
Retention policies + retention labels + records management. Keep, delete, and manage content lifecycle.
Legal + Audit
eDiscovery: search, hold, collect, review, and export content for legal proceedings and investigations.
Insider Risk
Insider risk management + communication compliance. Behavioral detection of risky insider activities.
Azure Governance
Azure Policy + resource locks + Blueprints + management groups. Enforce standards on Azure resources.
