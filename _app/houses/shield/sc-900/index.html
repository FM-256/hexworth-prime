SC-900 Exam Objectives

  • Describe the shared responsibility model and how it applies to cloud security
  • Explain the concepts of defense in depth and Zero Trust
  • Describe the CIA triad and common security threats including phishing, ransomware, and DDoS
  • Explain encryption concepts including symmetric, asymmetric, and hashing
  • Describe Microsoft Entra ID concepts including tenants, users, groups, and roles
  • Explain authentication methods including MFA, passwordless, and FIDO2
  • Describe Conditional Access policies and their role in Zero Trust
  • Explain identity governance concepts including PIM, access reviews, and entitlement management
  • Describe Microsoft Defender for Cloud, Defender for Endpoint, and Defender for Office 365
  • Explain Microsoft Sentinel as a cloud-native SIEM and SOAR solution
  • Describe Azure network security services including DDoS Protection, Firewall, WAF, NSGs, and Bastion
  • Explain Microsoft Purview compliance portal, Compliance Manager, and compliance score
  • Describe data classification, sensitivity labels, and data loss prevention (DLP)
  • Explain records management, retention policies, and eDiscovery
  • Describe insider risk management, communication compliance, and Azure resource governance
