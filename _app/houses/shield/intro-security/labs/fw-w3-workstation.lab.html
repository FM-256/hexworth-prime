Hardening Priority Ranker

Rank 12 workstation hardening steps from highest to lowest priority.
Use the arrows or drag and drop to reorder.

Scenario: A company just deployed 200 new Windows workstations. You are the security lead. Budget and time are limited -- you can only implement these steps in sequence. Rank them so the most critical protections come first.
Scoring: Each item earns points based on how close it is to the optimal position (0 = exact, penalty for distance).
Exact position = 2 pts Off by 1 = 1 pt Off by 2+ = 0 pts Max: 24 pts
