Phishing Triage

Examine 8 email mockups. For each, decide if it is legitimate or phishing.
For phishing emails, identify the red flags.

Dual scoring: You earn 1 point for the correct verdict (legit vs. phishing) and up to 1 bonus point for correctly identifying red flags on phishing emails.
