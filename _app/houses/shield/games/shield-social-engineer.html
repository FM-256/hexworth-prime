Social Engineer

Turn-Based Attack & Defense Strategy Game

Welcome to the Social Engineering Battlefield

In this turn-based strategy game, you'll experience both sides of social engineering attacks.

How to Play:

  • Attack Turns: Craft social engineering attacks using your budget. Choose attack vectors like phishing, vishing, smishing, or physical intrusion. More sophisticated attacks cost more but have higher success rates.
  • Defense Turns: Analyze incoming attacks and decide if they're legitimate or malicious. Deploy defensive measures using your security budget. Build up your defenses with training, tools, and policies.
  • Resource Management: Balance your attack budget and security budget. Earn more resources by successfully attacking and defending.
  • Progressive Difficulty: Early rounds feature obvious attacks. Later rounds combine multiple vectors in sophisticated multi-stage campaigns.
  • Win Condition: Score 1000 points before your security rating drops to 0.

Educational Goal: Learn real-world social engineering techniques and countermeasures through strategic gameplay.
