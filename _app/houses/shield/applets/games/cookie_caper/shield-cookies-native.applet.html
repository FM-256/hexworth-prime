Defense Tips: Protect Your Cookies

  • View cookies in Chrome: DevTools (F12) → Application tab → Cookies in left sidebar
  • Delete specific cookies: Right-click any cookie in DevTools → Delete, or click the clear icon
  • Block third-party cookies: Chrome Settings → Privacy → Third-party cookies → Block
  • Use HTTPS only: Install "HTTPS Everywhere" or enable Chrome's "Always use secure connections"
  • Clear tracking cookies regularly: Settings → Privacy → Clear browsing data → Cookies
  • Use private browsing: Incognito mode auto-deletes cookies when you close the window
  • Check cookie flags: Secure, HttpOnly, and SameSite flags protect against theft and CSRF
