Stage 1 — Triage the Indicators

Six artifacts were collected during initial containment of the Veridian Regional Hospital breach. For each one, select (a) the correct IOC type and (b) the MITRE ATT&CK tactic it maps to. Both fields must be correct to score the row. A naive guess (e.g., calling every row "Network" or "Execution") will not score enough rows to advance.

Objective: correctly classify all 6 indicators. You need all 6 correct to proceed.

Source: MITRE ATT&CK Enterprise v15 · SY0-701 objective 2.4 (indicators of malicious activity)

Stage 2 — Map Behaviors to ATT&CK Technique IDs

You've categorized the indicators. Now map each observed behavior to its precise MITRE ATT&CK sub-technique or technique ID. Technique IDs are specific (e.g., T1566.002 is Spearphishing Link, not all of T1566). Mismatching behaviors with the wrong technique fails the row even if the tactic was right in Stage 1.

Objective: match all 6 behaviors to the correct ATT&CK technique IDs. All 6 must be correct.

Source: MITRE ATT&CK Enterprise v15. All IDs are verified against the live ATT&CK knowledge base.

Stage 3 — Attribute the Threat Actor

You have assembled the TTP profile. Review the confirmed evidence, then select the actor classification that best fits the sophistication level, persistence, targeting profile, and motive. Only one classification is consistent with all the evidence.

Objective: select the correct threat-actor classification, then submit. A wrong choice is rejected with a rationale.
