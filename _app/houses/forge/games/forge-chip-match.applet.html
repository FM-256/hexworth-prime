CHIP MATCH

ASSEMBLY LINE

The factory floor is overflowing with components. Match sets of identical parts to build complete systems and clear the production queue.

WORK ORDER

Match 3 or more adjacent components horizontally or vertically. Chain reactions earn combo multipliers. Special parts unlock from larger matches.

OBJECTIVES

Keep the UPS charged while meeting production quotas. Matches restore power. Each level drains faster. Ten levels to master certification.

CONTROLS

SPECIAL COMPONENTS

SURGE COMPONENT (4-Match): Clears entire row or column when matched. Direction depends on match orientation.

EMP CHIP (5-Match): When activated, clears all components of the same type from the board.

SHORT CIRCUIT (L/T): Clears a 3×3 area around the matched component.

SCORING

• 3-match: 50 pts (+6% power)
• 4-match: 100 pts (+12% power) + Surge
• 5-match: 200 pts (+22% power) + EMP
• L/T shape: 150 pts (+18% power) + Short Circuit
• Combos multiply score and restore bonus power!
