Task 1: Traditional vs Modern Management

For each management scenario, determine whether it describes a traditional (on-premises AD/GPO/SCCM) approach or a modern (Azure AD/Intune/cloud) approach. Understanding the distinction is fundamental to MD-101.

Scenario A
Devices must be on the corporate network or connected via VPN to receive Group Policy updates and login authentication.
Scenario B
A remote worker opens their laptop at a coffee shop. Configuration policies are pushed automatically over the internet without VPN.
Scenario C
IT creates a golden image with SCCM, deploys it over the LAN via PXE boot, then manages updates through WSUS.
Scenario D
New laptops ship directly to employees. On first boot, they sign in with their work email and the device auto-enrolls in management, receives apps, and applies security policies.
Scenario E
Security baselines are enforced through Organizational Units and linked GPOs. Changes require replication across domain controllers.
Scenario F
IT enforces device compliance by checking encryption status, OS version, and antivirus state through a cloud portal. Non-compliant devices are blocked from accessing corporate email.

Task 2: Azure AD Join Scenarios

Azure AD offers three join types: Azure AD Joined (cloud-only), Hybrid Azure AD Joined (dual on-prem AD + Azure AD), and Azure AD Registered (BYOD/personal). Select the correct join type for each scenario.

Scenario A
A cloud-first startup purchases new Surface laptops. No on-premises servers exist. All users authenticate through Microsoft 365 and devices are managed entirely through Intune.
Scenario B
A large hospital has 2,000 domain-joined workstations managed by SCCM and GPO. They want to begin using Intune and Conditional Access while maintaining their existing AD infrastructure.
Scenario C
An employee wants to access company email and Teams on their personal Windows laptop. IT should not have full device control, but needs to protect corporate data with app protection policies.
Scenario D
A law firm is migrating from on-premises Exchange to Microsoft 365. During transition, desktops remain domain-joined but must also appear in Azure AD for Conditional Access enforcement.
Scenario E
A retail kiosk running Windows 10 needs to be fully managed through Intune. It has no need for on-premises domain services and connects to the internet via Wi-Fi.

Task 6: Co-Management Strategy

Co-management allows SCCM and Intune to jointly manage Windows devices. For each workload, decide whether to keep it in SCCM (pilot phase) or slide it to Intune (cloud-ready phase) based on the scenario.

Workload A: Compliance Policies
Organization wants to use Conditional Access to gate email access based on device compliance. Compliance evaluation must happen in the cloud for remote workers.
Workload B: Resource Access (VPN/Wi-Fi/Email Profiles)
Company has complex on-premises RADIUS/NPS infrastructure for certificate-based Wi-Fi auth. Not yet ready to move certificates to cloud PKI.
Workload C: Windows Update Policies
Organization wants to leverage Windows Update for Business rings and eliminate on-premises WSUS infrastructure. All devices have reliable internet access.
Workload D: Client Apps
IT has extensive SCCM task sequences for deploying complex legacy Win32 apps with custom scripts. These apps are not yet packaged for Intune Win32 app format.
Workload E: Device Configuration
Organization is fully cloud-ready. All new devices are Azure AD joined. IT wants to manage settings profiles through the Intune admin center for both remote and on-site workers.
