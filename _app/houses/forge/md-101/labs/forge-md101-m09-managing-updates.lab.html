Task 6: Troubleshoot Windows Update Failures

Failure A: Error 0x80073712

Failure B: Feature Update Rolls Back

A Windows 10 22H2 feature update starts installing, reaches 75%, and then automatically rolls back to the previous version. The device is a Dell Latitude with 128 GB SSD, 4 GB RAM, and 8 GB free disk space. The rollback happens on 3 identical models.

Failure C: Updates Stuck at "Pending Restart"

50 devices in the "Broad" update ring show updates in "Pending Restart" state for over 2 weeks. Users report they have not been prompted to restart. The update ring has a 5-day deadline configured, and active hours are set to 8 AM - 5 PM.
