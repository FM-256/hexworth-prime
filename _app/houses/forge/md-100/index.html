About This Course

The MD-100 exam measures your ability to deploy Windows clients, manage identity and access, manage compliance policies, and manage and protect devices. This course covers Windows 10 and Windows 11 client administration for the Microsoft 365 Certified: Modern Desktop Administrator certification.

Each module includes a presentation for learning, a hands-on walkthrough lab, an interactive sim lab for realistic practice, and a quiz for assessment. A midterm checkpoint with a 25-objective simulation lab and Jeopardy-style review game sits between M06 and M07. Three review games provide midterm, final, and comprehensive exam prep. Complete all 45 activities to master the curriculum.
