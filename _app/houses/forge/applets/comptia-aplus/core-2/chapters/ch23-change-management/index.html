Learning Objectives

  • Explain the purpose and importance of change management in IT operations
  • Differentiate between standard, normal, and emergency changes
  • Describe the role of the Change Advisory Board (CAB)
  • Complete a Request for Change (RFC) with required information
  • Conduct impact analysis and risk assessment for proposed changes
  • Develop and execute rollback plans when changes fail
  • Utilize sandbox and test environments for safe validation
  • Document changes and obtain end-user acceptance
