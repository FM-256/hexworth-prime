📝 Why Documentation Matters

Good documentation is the foundation of effective IT support and operations.

Benefits of Proper Documentation

  • Consistency: Everyone follows the same procedures
  • Training: New team members get up to speed faster
  • Troubleshooting: Historical data helps diagnose recurring issues
  • Compliance: Required for audits and regulatory requirements
  • Continuity: Knowledge isn't lost when employees leave
  • Accountability: Clear record of who did what and when
Reality Check: Poor documentation costs organizations millions in downtime, repeated mistakes, and lost productivity. Document as you work!

📦 Asset Inventory & Configuration

Asset Documentation Elements

Hardware Assets

  • Serial numbers and asset tags
  • Make, model, specifications
  • Purchase date and warranty
  • Location and assigned user
  • Maintenance history

Software Assets

  • License keys and counts
  • Version numbers
  • Installation dates
  • Renewal dates
  • Compliance status

Configuration Documentation

  • Baseline configurations: Standard settings for device types
  • Change history: Record all modifications with dates and reasons
  • Password vault: Secure storage for credentials (never in plaintext!)

📈 Escalation Procedures

Know when and how to escalate issues to higher support tiers.

Escalation Triggers

TriggerAction
Time-basedIssue exceeds SLA response/resolution time
Skill-basedIssue requires expertise beyond your level
Authority-basedDecision requires management approval
Severity-basedMajor outage affecting multiple users

Proper Escalation Includes

  • Complete documentation of all steps already taken
  • Clear explanation of why escalation is needed
  • All relevant logs, screenshots, and error messages
  • User contact information and availability
Key Point: Never escalate a blank ticket. Document everything you've tried so the next tier doesn't repeat your work.

📚 Knowledge Bases & Wikis

A knowledge base is a centralized repository of information for solving common issues.

Types of KB Articles

  • How-to guides: Step-by-step instructions for common tasks
  • Troubleshooting guides: Decision trees for diagnosing problems
  • FAQs: Answers to frequently asked questions
  • Known issues: Documented bugs with workarounds
  • Reference docs: Policies, procedures, contact lists

Writing Good KB Articles

  • Use clear, searchable titles
  • Include symptoms, cause, and solution
  • Add screenshots and examples
  • Keep language simple and jargon-free
  • Review and update regularly

💬 Professional Communication

Effective communication is as important as technical skills in IT support.

Key Communication Principles

  • Active listening: Let users finish, ask clarifying questions
  • Plain language: Avoid jargon; explain in terms users understand
  • Patience: Users may be frustrated - stay calm and helpful
  • Empathy: Acknowledge their frustration before solving
  • Set expectations: Be honest about timelines and capabilities

Do Say

  • "I understand this is frustrating"
  • "Let me explain what's happening"
  • "I'll keep you updated on progress"

Don't Say

  • "That's not my job"
  • "You should have known that"
  • "That's a dumb question"

👔 Professional Appearance & Attitude

Professional Appearance

  • Dress code: Follow company policy; when in doubt, dress up
  • ID badge: Always visible when on-site
  • Grooming: Clean, neat, and appropriate for workplace
  • Body language: Eye contact, confident posture, friendly demeanor

Professional Attitude

  • Punctuality: Be on time for appointments and meetings
  • Respect: Treat all users equally regardless of role
  • Accountability: Own your mistakes and learn from them
  • Confidentiality: Never discuss user issues with others
  • Positive attitude: Problems are opportunities to help
Remember: You represent the IT department. Every interaction shapes how users perceive IT.

De-escalation Techniques

  • Never argue or get defensive
  • Apologize for their inconvenience (not admitting fault)
  • Focus on what you CAN do, not what you can't
  • Know when to involve a supervisor

⏰ Time Management

Prioritization Matrix

PriorityImpactExample
Critical (P1)Business stoppedServer down, major outage
High (P2)Significant impactDepartment printer broken
Medium (P3)Limited impactIndividual software issue
Low (P4)Minimal impactHow-to question, enhancement request

Time Management Tips

  • Batch similar tasks: Handle all password resets together
  • Avoid multitasking: Complete one ticket before starting another
  • Block time: Reserve focus time for complex projects
  • Document as you go: Don't save it for later
  • Use templates: Pre-written responses for common issues
