1 Configure the Network Name (SSID)

The SSID (Service Set Identifier) is the name that identifies your wireless network. When devices search for available Wi-Fi networks, this is the name they'll see.

Best Practice: Choose a unique SSID that doesn't reveal personal information (like your name or address). Avoid default names like "NETGEAR" or "Linksys" as they may be targeted.

Your Task: Change the SSID from the default "NETGEAR-5G" to a custom name. Click in the SSID field above and enter a new network name.
