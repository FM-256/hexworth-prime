A Mobile Isolation Techniques (Objective 5.5)

Tests that isolate the real cause

  • 1Cross-device swap. Try the same cable/SIM/accessory on a known-good device, or a known-good cable/accessory on the faulty device. Whichever side still fails is where the fault lives.
  • 2Isolate the variable. Remove one factor at a time (case off, charger unplugged, protector removed) and re-observe before touching hardware.
  • 3Battery health check. Read the device's own battery-health/cycle-count report before assuming a software cause for battery symptoms.
  • 4Safety first. A visibly swollen battery is a fire/chemical hazard. Never press, bend, or puncture it. Power the device off and route it to certified service.

Why the order matters

  • 1A fix chosen before you isolate the cause is a guess. Replacing a battery when the fault is a setting wastes a repair.
  • 2A theory that ignores the evidence you gathered is still a guess, even if it sounds technical.
  • 3Testing separates a real cause from a plausible-sounding one. A disconfirmed theory sends you back to Theory, not forward to a fix.
  • 4The repair should directly address the CONFIRMED cause.
  • 5Never call a fault resolved until you have verified the device end to end.
