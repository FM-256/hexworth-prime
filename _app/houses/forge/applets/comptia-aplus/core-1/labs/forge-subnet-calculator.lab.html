Exam Tip: Quick CIDR Reference

/8 = 255.0.0.0 (Class A default) | /16 = 255.255.0.0 (Class B default) | /24 = 255.255.255.0 (Class C default)

Usable hosts formula: 2^(32 - CIDR) - 2 (subtract 2 for network and broadcast addresses)
