Cable Types

Straight Both ends SAME standard (B+B or A+A). Use between unlike devices: PC to switch, PC to wall jack.
Crossover One end T568A, other end T568B. Use between like devices: PC to PC, switch to switch (legacy, before Auto-MDIX).

Termination Procedure

  • 1Strip ~1 inch of outer jacket with a cable stripper. Do not nick the conductors.
  • 2Untwist each of the 4 pairs and fan all 8 wires flat.
  • 3Arrange wires in pin order 1–8 per your chosen standard.
  • 4Trim conductors even and seat firmly into the RJ45 plug.
  • 5Crimp the connector with a crimp tool to lock the contacts.
  • 6Test both ends with a cable tester. All 8 LEDs must light in order.
