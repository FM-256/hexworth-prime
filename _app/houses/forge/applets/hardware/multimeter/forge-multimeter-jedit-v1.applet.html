    Multimeter Quick Reference

    DC Voltage

    • PSU 12V rail: 11.4V – 12.6V
    • PSU 5V rail: 4.75V – 5.25V
    • PSU 3.3V rail: 3.135V – 3.465V
    • CMOS battery: 2.7V – 3.3V

    AC Voltage

    • North America: ~120V
    • Europe: ~230V
    • Frequency: 60Hz (NA)
    • Range: ±10% nominal

    Continuity

    • Good cable: < 5Ω
    • OL = Open circuit
    • Beep = conductive path
    • Test: 1 wire at a time

    Resistance

    • Start at highest range
    • Never test live circuits
    • RAM: 100kΩ – 2MΩ
    • OL = open/infinite
