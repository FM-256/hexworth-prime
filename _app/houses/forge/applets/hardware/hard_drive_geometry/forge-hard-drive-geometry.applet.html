What is Hard Drive Geometry?

Hard drive geometry describes how data is physically organized on the magnetic platters inside a traditional hard disk drive (HDD). Understanding geometry is essential for grasping how data is stored, addressed, and retrieved at the hardware level.

Key Point: Geometry determines the maximum storage capacity of a drive. It is defined by the number of platters, tracks per surface, and sectors per track.
