Ethics in Information Technology -- Wheel of Fortune Review

Eight ethics and professionalism concepts hidden behind letter tiles. Spin for consonants, buy vowels, solve the puzzle. Instructor-led classroom format: read the category aloud, let students call letters.

~25 min Game All Levels

How to Play

  • Instructor reads the category aloud -- students identify the hidden phrase one letter at a time
  • Click Spin to get a dollar value, then click a consonant to guess it. Each occurrence earns that spin value
  • Click Buy Vowel to spend $250 and reveal all occurrences of A, E, I, O, or U (vowels do not add spin value)
  • Click Solve at any time to guess the full phrase -- correct = puzzle won + $1000 bonus; wrong = locked for one turn (spin to unlock)
  • Reveal Puzzle is an instructor override when the class is ready to move on -- no score change
Scoring

Consonants: spin value x occurrences. Vowels: costs $250, no reward. Solve bonus: $1,000 on correct guess. Wrong solve: locked for one turn (spin/buy only). Max score depends on spin luck.
