Course Objectives

  • Discuss the intersection of Internet use and ethical values (Spinello Ch. 1).
  • Consider regulation and governance of cyberspace and the security professional's organizational role (Spinello Ch. 2).
  • Discuss free speech and censorship in cyberspace (Spinello Ch. 3).
  • Categorize intellectual property concerns and rights in cyberspace (Spinello Ch. 4).
  • Understand privacy rights in cyberspace (Spinello Ch. 5).
  • Define and discuss cybercrime, crime actors, and cybersecurity as a moral obligation (Spinello Ch. 6).
  • Review and discuss ethical issues presented in the US Cyberspace Solarium Commission's current work.
  • Participate in case studies (EDT labs) that probe cyberethical issues affecting public and private organizations.
  • Map course content to NIST CSF Functions: ID.BE, ID.GV, PR.AT, PR.IP.
