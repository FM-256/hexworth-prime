Phishing Analyzer

Examine email messages and identify phishing attempts along with their red flags

Lab 6 25 minutes 8 Emails

Instructions

Each card below displays an email with sender, subject, and body. For each email:

1. Decide whether it is Legitimate or Phishing.

2. If you mark it as Phishing, select all red flags that apply from the checklist.

Scoring: 2 points per correct verdict, plus 1 point per correctly identified flag (no penalty for wrong flags on legitimate emails). You need 70% of possible points to unlock Mark Complete.
