Instructions

Step 1: Drag the business functions into order of criticality (most critical at the top).

Step 2: Assign an RTO (Recovery Time Objective) to each function using the dropdown.

Step 3: Select a DR strategy (Hot / Warm / Cold / Cloud) for each function.

Click Check Plan to validate your decisions. Scoring is based on correct rank position, appropriate RTO assignment, and suitable DR strategy selection. You need 70% to unlock Mark Complete.
