The 8 Saltzer & Schroeder Principles

  • Economy of Mechanism -- Keep designs simple and small.
  • Fail-Safe Defaults -- Default to deny; access must be explicitly granted.
  • Complete Mediation -- Check every access to every object.
  • Open Design -- Security should not depend on secrecy of the mechanism.
  • Separation of Privilege -- Require multiple conditions to grant access.
  • Least Privilege -- Grant only the minimum access necessary.
  • Least Common Mechanism -- Minimize shared mechanisms between users.
  • Psychological Acceptability -- Security mechanisms must be easy to use correctly.
