Company Profile: NovaByte Retail

NovaByte Retail is a mid-size e-commerce company that operates an online storefront and 12 physical locations. Review the profile details below -- you will use them to determine compliance status for each control.

  • Industry: Retail / E-commerce
  • Payment processing: Accepts credit cards online and in-store
  • Infrastructure: Hybrid cloud (AWS + on-premises data center)
  • Employee count: 340 employees
  • Encryption at rest: Enabled for cloud databases only
  • Encryption in transit: TLS 1.2 on all public endpoints
  • Access control: Role-based access, no MFA for internal users
  • Logging: Cloud audit logs enabled; on-prem logs not centralized
  • Pen testing: Last external test was 18 months ago
  • Incident response: Documented IR plan, tested via tabletop 6 months ago
  • Vendor management: No formal third-party risk assessment program
  • Security training: Annual awareness training, no role-based training

Instructions

For each control statement below, make two decisions based on the company profile:

1. Verdict: Is NovaByte currently Compliant or Non-Compliant with this control?

2. Framework: Which framework requirement does this control map to?

You earn 1 point per correct verdict and 1 point per correct framework mapping (20 points max). You need 16/20 to unlock Mark Complete.
