Course Objectives

  • 1. Review cybersecurity basics, concepts, terms, origins, and "security by design"
  • 2. Recognize the need for cybersecurity programs to manage risks from technical defects, human behavior, or natural disasters
  • 3. Understand basics of cybersecurity management, governance terms/processes, and business processes
  • 4. Explain basics of risk management models and techniques
  • 5. Recognize organizational cybersecurity relationships, roles, and collaboration opportunities
  • 6. Describe components of NIST standards -- SP 800-53 and the Cybersecurity Framework (CSF)
