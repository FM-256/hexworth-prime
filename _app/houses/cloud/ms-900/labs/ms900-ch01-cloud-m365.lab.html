Stay informed about upcoming changes, new features, and maintenance across Microsoft 365 services
Plan for Change Teams: Updated meeting recording storage policy Apr 21, 2026
Starting May 15, 2026, new meeting recordings will be stored in OneDrive instead of Stream. Existing recordings remain accessible. Review and update any links in documentation or SharePoint pages.
New Feature Copilot for Microsoft 365 — General Availability Apr 18, 2026
Microsoft 365 Copilot is now generally available for Business Premium and E3/E5 tenants. Admins can control Copilot access through the Microsoft 365 admin center under Settings > Copilot.
Action Required Basic Authentication retirement — additional services Apr 15, 2026
Basic authentication will be disabled for SMTP AUTH and additional protocols on June 1, 2026. Verify that all applications and devices use modern authentication before this date.
Stay Informed Exchange Online: Updated anti-spoofing policies Apr 12, 2026
Microsoft has updated default anti-spoofing policies in Exchange Online Protection. Tenant admins should review their custom anti-phishing policy configurations to ensure alignment with the new defaults.
