MS-900 Exam Objectives

  • Describe cloud computing concepts including public, private, and hybrid models
  • Identify Microsoft 365 components: Office apps, Exchange Online, SharePoint, Teams, OneDrive
  • Compare Microsoft 365 subscription plans across Business, Enterprise, and Frontline tiers
  • Explain the benefits of cloud services for organizations including scalability and HA
  • Describe Microsoft Entra ID concepts: authentication, MFA, and Conditional Access
  • Identify Microsoft 365 Defender services: Defender for Endpoint, Office 365, Identity, Cloud Apps
  • Describe Microsoft Purview capabilities: compliance, DLP, and information protection
  • Explain Microsoft privacy principles, Trust Center, and Service Trust Portal
  • Describe Microsoft 365 pricing models: per-user, monthly, and annual licensing
  • Explain licensing options and plan comparisons across Microsoft 365 tiers
  • Navigate the Microsoft 365 admin center for billing and usage reports
  • Identify support options: self-service, community forums, and Microsoft support plans
  • Describe the Modern Lifecycle Policy, feature deprecation, and update channels
  • Explain how Microsoft 365 supports collaboration, mobility, and modern workplace scenarios
