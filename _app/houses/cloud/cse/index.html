About This Course

The EC-Council Cloud Security Engineer (CSE v1) certification validates expertise in securing cloud environments across all major providers. This course covers the full spectrum of cloud security disciplines: from foundational concepts and identity management through data protection, network hardening, application security, and incident response, culminating in risk management and regulatory compliance.

Each module includes a presentation, hands-on lab, and quiz. A comprehensive review ties all 8 modules together. Complete all 25 activities to master the CSE curriculum.
