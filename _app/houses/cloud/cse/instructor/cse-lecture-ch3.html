Chapter 3 · EC-Council C|CSE

Identity and Access

The control plane has one front door, and it is identity.

Vocabulary first, and be strict about it

IAM discussions collapse when these words blur. Hold the line all chapter.

Authentication: who are you Authorisation: what may you do Passing the first is not passing the second

Most identities are not people

The census surprises students every time.

Machines outnumber people many times over They hold most of the privilege None of them can carry MFA

Least privilege is engineering, not aspiration

Every student will nod at the principle. Teach the practice.

Start at zero, not at convenient Add a permission only on evidence Every key you keep is a key that can leak

The IAM failures that actually cause breaches

Close on the failure catalogue — it maps to the lab.

Long-lived keys nobody rotates Wildcard permissions Admin accounts without MFA A leaked key IS the breach
