Lab 2 Quantitative Risk Analysis (ALE Calculation)

Objective: Calculate Annualized Loss Expectancy (ALE) using Single Loss Expectancy (SLE) and Annualized Rate of Occurrence (ARO) to quantify financial risk.
ALE = SLE × ARO
ALE: Annualized Loss Expectancy | SLE: Single Loss Expectancy | ARO: Annualized Rate of Occurrence

Available Commands

risk-calc sle --asset --impact Calculate Single Loss Expectancy
risk-calc aro --threat-history Determine Annualized Rate of Occurrence
risk-calc ale --comprehensive Calculate full ALE with recommendations
Risk Calculation Terminal - Quantitative Analysis
risk-analyst@cloud-soc:~$ risk-calc sle --asset customer-database --impact breach
