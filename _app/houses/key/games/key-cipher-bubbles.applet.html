MISSION BRIEF

Rogue ciphertexts are flooding the crypto lab. Trap them in bubbles and pop them before they escape. Each wave brings stronger cryptographic algorithms — reversible ciphers you decrypt, and a one-way hash you cannot.

AES - Armored, takes 2 pops
RSA - Heavy, slow but tough
MD5 - Deprecated one-way HASH (not a cipher)
