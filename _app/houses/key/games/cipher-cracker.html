Mission Briefing

Agent, we've intercepted 10 encrypted enemy transmissions. Your mission is to decrypt each message using various classical cipher techniques.

Speed and accuracy are critical. Each successful decryption earns you points, with bonuses for quick work. Use hints sparingly—they cost you points.

Scoring: 100 points per correct decode + up to 50 time bonus - 25 per hint used

Your country is counting on you. Good luck, Agent.
