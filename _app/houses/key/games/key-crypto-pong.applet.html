CRYPTO PONG

CIPHER BRIEF

The ball carries encrypted data. Your paddle (ENCRYPT) must deflect it past the enemy's paddle (DECRYPT). Each rally adds another layer of encryption.

CRYPTO MECHANICS

The ball transforms between plaintext and ciphertext as it bounces. Hit the ball at the paddle edge for spin. Powerups alter the encryption algorithm, changing ball behavior.
