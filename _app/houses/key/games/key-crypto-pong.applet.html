CRYPTO PONG

CIPHER BRIEF

Every serve shows a real ciphertext, its cipher name, and its key. Your paddle has three bands: TOP, MID, BOTTOM. Decode the ciphertext, then position your paddle so the band showing the correct plaintext is what meets the ball.

CRYPTO MECHANICS

Correct band = the ball returns. Wrong band, or a miss, and the ball passes straight through untouched. The cipher escalates each round: Caesar, then Atbash, then XOR, then Vigenere.
