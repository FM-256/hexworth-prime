SOHO Network Builder

Drag network components onto the floor plan to build a working SOHO network

Requirements

  • Place a Modem to provide internet connectivity
  • Place a Router connected to the modem for NAT and routing
  • Place a Switch to connect wired devices
  • Place a Wireless AP for Wi-Fi coverage
  • Place at least 2 PCs and 1 Printer as endpoints
  • The validator checks device presence and logical connectivity
