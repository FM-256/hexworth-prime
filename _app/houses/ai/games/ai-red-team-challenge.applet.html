AI Red Team Challenge

ADVERSARIAL PROMPTING & AI SAFETY ASSESSMENT

You are a red team operator auditing AI systems for safety vulnerabilities. Your job is to probe each deployed AI for weaknesses — and then design the defenses that would have stopped your own attacks.

This training covers real-world adversarial techniques catalogued by AI safety researchers: prompt injection, jailbreaks, encoding bypasses, context manipulation, and more. Understanding how attacks work is the first step to building systems that resist them.

Warning: These are educational simulations. Techniques demonstrated here should only be used against systems you are authorized to test.
