Core Concepts

This module covers the essential theory and practical techniques of ML Supply Chain in adversarial machine learning. Understanding these concepts is critical for both attacking and defending production ML systems.

Key Insight

Adversarial ML attacks are not theoretical -- they have been demonstrated against real production systems including malware detectors, face recognition, spam filters, and financial fraud models. Understanding the attack mechanics is a prerequisite for building meaningful defenses.

Attack PerspectiveUnderstanding how attacks are constructed, what assumptions they require, and what their computational cost is informs which deployment contexts are most at risk.
Defense PerspectiveEffective defenses must be evaluated against adaptive adversaries who know the defense. Security claims require formal guarantees or empirical robustness evaluations against strong attacks.

Evaluation Discipline

Always evaluate defenses against adaptive adversaries who know the defense mechanism. A defense that appears robust against standard PGD may be bypassed by an attack specifically designed to circumvent it -- a phenomenon known as "obfuscated gradients."
