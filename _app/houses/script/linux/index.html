About This Course

Linux Administration takes you from first boot to advanced system operation. You will build practical command-line fluency, understand the Unix philosophy, and develop the hands-on skills demanded by roles in system administration, DevOps, and cybersecurity. The course maps directly to the CompTIA Linux+ (XK0-005) exam objectives.

Each chapter includes a slide presentation, hands-on lab, and knowledge quiz. A comprehensive Jeopardy review ties all 12 chapters together. Complete all 37 activities to master the full curriculum.
