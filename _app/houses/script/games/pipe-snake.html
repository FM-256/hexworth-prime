PIPE SNAKE

MAN PAGE

You are PID-1, the init process. Chain commands together with pipes to build the longest pipeline in /proc history. But beware: if you pipe into yourself, it's a segfault.

PIPING RULES

Collect commands (ls, grep, awk, sed...) to grow your pipeline. Each command adds power. Special tokens grant abilities. Avoid walls, your own tail, and zombie processes.
