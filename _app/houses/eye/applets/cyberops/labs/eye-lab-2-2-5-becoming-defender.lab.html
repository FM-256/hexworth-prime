🗺️ Part 3: Career Roadmap

Typical SOC Career Progression

Step 1: Build Foundation

Get certified (Security+, CySA+), learn networking, practice in home labs.

0-6 months

Step 2: Entry-Level SOC Analyst

Alert triage, ticket management, basic incident handling, tool familiarization.

6-18 months

Step 3: Advanced Analysis

Deep investigations, detection tuning, mentoring juniors, specialty development.

2-4 years

Step 4: Specialization

Choose path: Threat Hunting, Detection Engineering, Incident Response Lead, or Management.

4+ years
