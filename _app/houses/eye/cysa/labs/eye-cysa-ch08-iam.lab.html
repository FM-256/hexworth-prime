CySA+ Insight: RBAC is the dominant model in enterprise environments because job functions map cleanly to roles. ABAC adds dynamism (e.g., "Finance Analyst can access records only from corporate network, 9am-6pm") but requires more complex policy engines. The exam will often describe a scenario and ask you to identify the access control model — focus on what drives the access decision: role, label, attribute, or owner permission.

Key Concept: FIDO2 hardware keys (YubiKey, etc.) are phishing-resistant because they use origin-bound cryptographic attestation — the key response is tied to the legitimate domain. A phishing site cannot replay the credential because the origin check fails. SMS and TOTP are vulnerable to real-time MITM phishing because the code is domain-agnostic. Push notifications are vulnerable to fatigue attacks (Uber breach 2022).
CySA+ Insight: The five MFA factor categories on the CySA+ exam are: (1) Knowledge — something you know (password, PIN, security questions), (2) Possession — something you have (phone, smart card, hardware token), (3) Biometric — something you are (fingerprint, retina, face), (4) Location — somewhere you are (geofencing, trusted IP range), (5) Behavior/Context — how you behave (typing cadence, device posture). True MFA requires factors from at least two DIFFERENT categories.

Account Lifecycle Phases

Review the lifecycle phases below. The audit found failures at multiple stages. Identify which lifecycle phase was skipped or failed for each finding above.

+
1. Provisioning
Account creation with minimum necessary privileges based on job role. Approval workflow required for privileged access. Service accounts get dedicated, unique credentials.
↻
2. Maintenance & Role Changes
When employees change roles, previous privileges are revoked before new privileges are granted. User access reviews (UAR) performed quarterly. No accumulated rights across roles.
3. Periodic Access Review
Formal quarterly review of all privileged accounts. Managers certify that direct reports still require their current access level. Results documented for compliance.
⊗
4. Offboarding & Termination
Account disabled within 24 hours of termination. All access tokens, VPN certs, and API keys revoked. Shared credentials rotated immediately. HR-to-IT process SLA enforced.
PAM Best Practices Checklist

Check each PAM control that Meridian should implement to remediate the findings above.

Real-World Note: The Uber breach (2022) involved an attacker purchasing compromised VPN credentials for a contractor whose access was not properly offboarded. Combined with MFA fatigue, this yielded full internal access. Both privilege creep and orphaned accounts are among the top IAM failure modes in breach post-mortems.

CySA+ Insight: These four alerts represent the most common IAM attack sequence in modern breaches: (1) credential compromise, (2) Kerberos ticket abuse for lateral movement, (3) orphaned account exploitation for exfiltration, (4) LDAP/BloodHound enumeration for privilege escalation planning. On the exam, know that Golden Ticket attacks require the krbtgt hash, Pass-the-Ticket uses stolen TGT/TGS tickets, and LDAP enumeration with BloodHound is reconnaissance — not yet an attack but a critical indicator of a sophisticated threat actor.
