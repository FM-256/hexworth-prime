Exercise 3: DNS Enumeration

Use dig and nslookup to enumerate DNS records. Try: dig MX meridiantech.com, nslookup -type=TXT meridiantech.com, dig axfr meridiantech.com.

MX records reveal mail servers. TXT records often contain SPF/DKIM data. AXFR is a zone transfer attempt — often blocked but worth trying.
DNS Terminal // dig / nslookup
// Commands: dig [TYPE] meridiantech.com | nslookup -type=[TYPE] meridiantech.com
$
