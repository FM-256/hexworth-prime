Hexworth Web Flasher

A single entry point for flashing or installing software on every Hexworth-supported hardware platform: ESP32 (in-browser), Raspberry Pi Pico (drag-drop), Arduino (IDE handoff), and Raspberry Pi OS (Imager walkthrough). You own the device — Hexworth provides the instruction and software.

This browser cannot flash ESP32 devices in-place.

In-browser flashing for ESP32 requires the WebSerial API, available in Chrome, Edge, and Opera on desktop. Safari, Firefox, and mobile browsers do not support it.

The Raspberry Pi Pico, Arduino, and Raspberry Pi OS paths below work in any browser — they use downloads and external tools instead of WebSerial.

4. Provision & (optional) pair

  1. On your phone, join the WiFi network named HexworthDevice-XXXX (the XXXX is the last 4 of the device's MAC).
  2. A captive portal opens. Pick your 2.4 GHz home WiFi and enter the password.
  3. Optional: paste your Hexworth pairing code (see panel below). Without a code, the device registers anonymously and stays admin-invisible to your account.
  4. Submit. The device saves the settings and reboots. It will appear in your My Devices dashboard within a few seconds.
