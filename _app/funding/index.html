Funding Hub

Discover grants, scholarships, and financial resources for cybersecurity education. Whether you are a student seeking tuition support, an educator building a program, or a small business investing in cyber resilience -- the funding is out there.

Students Educators Small Business Institutions
Quick Tips for Funding Success
Start 6+ months before deadlines -- most grants require extensive prep
Build relationships with program officers before submitting
Align proposals with the funder's strategic priorities, not just your needs
Include measurable outcomes and a realistic evaluation plan
Budget for external evaluation -- it strengthens credibility
Read winning proposals when available (NSF has a public database)

Federal Grants

Major U.S. federal funding programs supporting cybersecurity education, workforce development, and academic research.

Typical Award Range $50K - $5M
Primary Agencies NSF, NSA/DHS, DoD, DOE, NIST
Deadline Pattern Annual cycles, Oct-Jan peak
Eligibility Accredited institutions, nonprofits

State Grants

State-level funding sources with emphasis on Florida programs. Check your state workforce board for equivalent opportunities.

Typical Award Range $5K - $3M
Key Programs CTE, CareerSource, WIOA, Job Growth
Deadline Pattern Rolling to annual, varies by state
Eligibility State residents, public institutions, training providers

Private & Industry Funding

Industry associations, vendor programs, and private foundations investing in the cybersecurity talent pipeline.

Typical Award Range $1K - $50K (or in-kind)
Key Sources ISC2, CompTIA, SANS, AWS, Google, Microsoft
Deadline Pattern Rolling, cohort-based, or annual
Eligibility Educators, institutions, nonprofits, students

Small Business Cyber Grants

Funding to help small and medium businesses strengthen their cybersecurity posture. Includes federal programs, state initiatives, and industry resources.

Typical Award Range $5K - $500K
Key Programs SBA, NIST MEP, SBIR/STTR, State Cyber
Deadline Pattern Rolling to quarterly solicitations
Eligibility U.S. small businesses (500 employees or fewer)

Institutional Grants

Funding for equipment, lab infrastructure, curriculum development, and program capacity building at educational institutions.

Typical Award Range $25K - $2M
Key Areas Lab equipment, curriculum, faculty dev, accreditation
Deadline Pattern Annual, aligned with academic calendar
Eligibility Accredited colleges, universities, K-12 districts

Scholarships

Cybersecurity-focused scholarships including programs for women in tech, veterans, underrepresented groups, and general merit-based awards.

Typical Award Range $1K - Full Tuition + Stipend
Key Programs CyberCorps SFS, WiCyS, AFCEA, DoD CySP
Deadline Pattern Jan-Apr peak, some rolling
Eligibility Students in cyber/CS programs, U.S. citizens (for gov programs)
