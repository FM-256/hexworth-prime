Trust & Transparency

How Hexworth Prime protects you and your data

About Hexworth Prime

Hexworth Prime is a comprehensive IT and cybersecurity education platform. Built by an educator with extensive industry and intelligence experience, it provides 3,500+ interactive modules across 12 learning houses covering networking, security, cloud, forensics, AI, hardware, and more.

The platform features over 120 CTF boxes, 120 Dojo vulnerability challenges, 202 portfolio-ready projects (100 software + 102 hardware), a 120-mission coding system spanning 7 languages, live tournament streaming (The Panopticon), real-time messaging (The Ansible), 12 AI house advisors, and server-side graded assessments. All browser-based, zero-install.

Infrastructure Enterprise-Grade

Hexworth Prime is hosted on secure, enterprise-grade infrastructure:

  • Firebase Hosting - Google's globally distributed CDN for fast, reliable content delivery
  • Google Cloud Platform - Industry-leading cloud infrastructure with built-in DDoS protection
  • 99.95% SLA - Guaranteed uptime backed by Google's service level agreement
  • HTTPS Everywhere - All connections are encrypted via TLS 1.3

Authentication

Hexworth Prime uses Google Authentication exclusively for user sign-in:

  • We never store your password - authentication is handled entirely by Google
  • We only receive your email address and display name from Google
  • No username/password creation required - use your existing Google account
  • Benefit from Google's industry-leading security including 2FA support

Data Handling

Your learning progress and preferences are stored securely:

  • Offline Mode (Default) - Progress is stored in your browser's localStorage. Your data never leaves your device unless you explicitly join a class.
  • Online Classes - When you join an instructor-led class, progress data syncs to Firestore (Google's secure database) so your instructor can track your learning journey.
  • You Control Your Data - You can clear your local progress anytime through Settings. Class data is managed by your instructor.

Privacy

Your privacy is paramount. Here's what we don't do:

  • No Third-Party Data Sharing - We never sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties
  • No Advertisements - The platform is completely ad-free
  • No Invasive Tracking - We only use basic analytics to improve the platform (page views, general usage patterns)
  • No Cookies for Marketing - Essential cookies only for functionality

FERPA Considerations

For educational institutions and instructors:

  • Student Data Protection - Student progress data is only visible to the instructor who created the class
  • Instructor-Controlled Classes - Instructors manage their own class rosters and can remove students at any time
  • Minimal Data Collection - We only collect what's necessary for learning progress tracking (completed modules, quiz scores)
  • No Directory Information Exposed - Student information is not shared across classes or made publicly visible
  • Data Retention - Class data can be deleted by the instructor at the end of a term

Questions?

If you have any questions about how Hexworth Prime handles your data, or if you need documentation for your institution's compliance requirements, please contact your instructor or institution administrator.
