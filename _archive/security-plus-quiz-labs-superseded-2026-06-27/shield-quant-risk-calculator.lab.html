Quantitative Risk Analysis Workshop

Compute SLE, ALE, and cost-benefit for six real-world risk scenarios. Each scenario gives you the asset value, exposure factor, and occurrence rate -- you run the numbers, then decide whether a proposed control is financially justified.

Formula Reference (SY0-701 Domain 5.0)
SLE = AV x EF  |  Asset Value x Exposure Factor (0-1, fraction of asset lost per incident)
ALE = SLE x ARO  |  SLE x Annualized Rate of Occurrence (events per year; 0.25 = once every 4 years)
ALE Reduction = ALE_before - ALE_after
Control is JUSTIFIED when: ALE Reduction > Annual Control Cost
Net Benefit = ALE Reduction - Annual Control Cost (negative = not worth it)
Progress: 0 / 6 scenarios graded
