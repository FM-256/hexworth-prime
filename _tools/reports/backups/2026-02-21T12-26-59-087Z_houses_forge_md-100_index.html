About This Course

The MD-100 exam measures your ability to deploy Windows clients, manage identity and access, manage compliance policies, and manage and protect devices. This course covers Windows 10 and Windows 11 client administration for the Microsoft 365 Certified: Modern Desktop Administrator certification.

Each module includes a presentation for learning, a hands-on lab for practice, and a quiz for assessment. Three Jeopardy-style review games provide midterm, final, and comprehensive exam prep. Complete all 36 activities to master the curriculum.
